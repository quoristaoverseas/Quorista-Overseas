Privacy

When you submit an enquiry, Quorista Overseas receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, interests, destination preference and enquiry message.

How we use enquiry information

We use submitted information to understand your request, contact you about the enquiry and provide relevant information about our services. Please do not submit passwords, financial account credentials or other highly sensitive information through the website form.

Form processing

The website enquiry form uses FormSubmit as the form delivery service. Your submission is transmitted to the email address configured for Quorista Overseas. FormSubmit states that form submissions are retained for a limited period and that spam-prevention systems may be used.
