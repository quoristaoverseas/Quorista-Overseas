Privacy Policy

How Quorista Overseas collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or career application, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, destination or role of interest, city, education or work details, links to your resume or profile, and any message you write.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide study, travel, scholarship or visa-related guidance.
  • To review and respond to career and internship applications.
  • To contact you by your preferred method (WhatsApp, phone or email).
  • To improve our website and services.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form-handling service, and we may share relevant details with institutions or authorities only when needed for an application you ask us to support.

4. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have shared by writing to quoristaoverseas@gmail.com.

6. Third-party links

Our site links to services such as WhatsApp and Instagram, which have their own privacy practices.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

8. Contact

Questions? Email quoristaoverseas@gmail.com.
