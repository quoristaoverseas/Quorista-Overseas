01 · OVERVIEW

Hungary at a glance

Explore English-taught education routes, Central European travel connections and the practical requirements for students and visitors.

Country context

Hungarian universities offer English-taught options in fields such as medicine, engineering, business, IT and sciences, but availability and entry standards vary by institution and course. The University of Debrecen, for example, advertises more than 80 fully English-taught degree programmes and publishes programme-level fees. Check the official course page for the intake, entrance examination, language evidence and full fee schedule. Stipendium Hungaricum eligibility is tied to the applicant’s sending country and approved study fields; it is not a universal scholarship.

02 · STUDY ABROAD

Study pathways in Hungary

Compare English-taught programmes, entry requirements, total costs and the right student-residence route before choosing a university.

A Bologna-aligned degree system

Hungary follows the European higher-education cycle: Bachelor’s, Master’s and Doctoral degrees. Programme length and entry requirements are set by each institution and may differ by subject.

  • Bachelor’s (BA/BSc): commonly 3–4 years (6–8 semesters); applicants need a secondary-school qualification that permits university entry in their home system.
  • Master’s (MA/MSc): commonly 1–2 years (2–4 semesters); a relevant recognised Bachelor’s degree is normally required.
  • Doctoral (PhD): commonly 4 years; research fit, academic record, proposal and supervisor/faculty requirements matter.
  • Undivided programmes: medicine and selected professional degrees may run as long one-tier programmes, often 5–6 years.

Preparatory or language courses are separate pathways. Confirm whether a course qualifies for a study residence permit before paying a deposit.

English-language preparation

IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT, Cambridge qualifications, PTE Academic, Duolingo or a prior English-medium qualification may be considered—but acceptance and minimum scores are programme-specific. Accepted tests, minimum scores and exemptions vary by programme; do not treat any score range as a national rule.

Some universities conduct their own online entrance tests or interviews. Medicine, dentistry, engineering and other selective programmes may have subject-specific examinations. Never promise an MOI waiver or a test exemption until the university confirms it in writing.

Scholarships: Stipendium Hungaricum

Stipendium Hungaricum is Hungary’s principal government scholarship programme. It can include tuition-fee coverage, a monthly stipend, a dormitory place or accommodation contribution, and health-insurance support. The stipend and housing contribution are intended as contributions to living costs—not a guarantee that all expenses will be covered. Dormitory places depend on institutional capacity.

Eligibility is determined by the current annual call and the applicant’s sending partner. For India, the official partner page identifies the University Grants Commission and lists broad eligible fields including natural and life sciences, IT, economics, business/management and engineering for full-degree Bachelor’s, Master’s and doctoral applications. This is not blanket eligibility for every programme. Applicants must check the current programme list, nomination rules and UGC process. Scholarship applications are separate from ordinary self-funded admission.

Timing: the 2026/27 application cycle is closed. Future applicants should monitor the official portal for the next call and deadline rather than rely on last year’s date.

06 · STUDENT IMMIGRATION

Student residence permit

For non-EU students, long-term study is handled through Hungary’s study-residence framework—not a tourist Schengen visa.

Purpose of studies

Applicants generally need acceptance from an eligible educational institution, evidence of the language ability needed for the course, a valid travel document, suitable accommodation, sufficient means of subsistence and health-insurance coverage. The competent Hungarian immigration authority (OIF) assesses the application and supporting evidence. The authority does not prescribe one universal €8,000–€12,000 bank-balance rule for every case; funds may be evidenced through personal resources, sponsor support, regular income or a scholarship, subject to the official criteria.

Applications from abroad are normally lodged through the competent Hungarian diplomatic or consular mission; the entry visa connected with an approved residence permit is handled through the mission. Follow the instructions for the applicant’s consular district and current procedure.

Work during studies

Under current OIF guidance, a third-country national holding a study residence permit may work up to 30 hours per week during the study period, and full-time for up to 90 days per year outside the study period. This is permission subject to the residence status and applicable rules—not a guarantee of a job or sufficient income.

After successfully completing studies, eligible graduates may apply for a residence permit to seek a job or start a business, subject to the authority’s conditions. Do not describe this as automatic post-study work permission.

07 · STUDENT LIFE

Living and working as a student

City comparison

  • Budapest: broadest choice of institutions and city amenities, larger international community and more internships; housing can be more expensive.
  • Debrecen: major university city with a strong medical and science community; compare housing and local employment options.
  • Szeged: university-centred southern city with a strong academic environment and walkable central areas.
  • Pécs: historic university city with a student-oriented atmosphere; compare programme-specific opportunities.
  • Győr: industrial and engineering-oriented location; verify the relevant English-taught course and campus.

Everyday practicalities

University dormitories, shared apartments and private studios are the main housing options. Student offices, orientation weeks, buddy schemes and international student groups can help with settling in. Hungarian is the official language; English is used widely in international university settings and central tourist districts, but learning basic Hungarian helps with daily life.

Health coverage must meet residence requirements. Confirm whether the university, scholarship or private policy provides the required comprehensive cover. Keep accommodation registration and residence documents current.

08 · INTERNATIONAL TOURISM

Explore Hungary

From Budapest’s Danube riverfront and thermal baths to historic towns and Lake Balaton, Hungary suits a relaxed city break with optional regional day trips.

Country snapshot

  • Capital: Budapest.
  • Currency: Hungarian forint (HUF). Cards are widely accepted, but keep some forints for small purchases.
  • Language: Hungarian; English is common in many Budapest hotels, restaurants and tourist areas, but less consistent outside major centres.
  • Time: Central European Time (UTC+1) in winter and Central European Summer Time (UTC+2) in summer.
  • Emergency: 112 for police, ambulance and fire services.
  • Best sightseeing periods: April–June and September–October are generally comfortable; July–August is busier and warmer, while December is known for Advent markets.

Tourist visa for Indian passport holders

Indian ordinary passport holders require a Schengen short-stay Type C visa to visit Hungary. The short-stay limit is up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen area. Apply through the correct Hungarian visa channel for your place of residence and itinerary. A visa is preliminary permission to travel to the border; it does not guarantee admission.

Prepare the current application form, passport, photograph, travel medical insurance meeting Schengen requirements, itinerary, accommodation evidence, financial evidence and other documents specified by the mission/VFS. Biometrics and an in-person appointment may be required. Do not treat the report’s processing times, fees, city list or VFS service charges as fixed: check the live official checklist and appointment channel before booking. Apply well ahead of travel and avoid non-refundable bookings before a decision.

11 · LOCAL TRANSPORT

Getting around

Budapest public transport

Metro, tram, bus and suburban rail make most central sightseeing straightforward. Buy and validate the correct ticket, or use a suitable travelcard. BKK’s published reference fares include HUF 500 for a single ticket bought in advance, HUF 2,500 for the 100E airport shuttle, HUF 2,750 for a 24-hour Budapest-travelcard and HUF 5,750 for a 72-hour travelcard. Check the current fare page before travel.

For airport transfers, compare the official 100E shuttle, public-transport routes and licensed taxis. App-based services and official taxi companies are preferable to unmarked vehicles.

12 · FOOD & DINING

Hungarian food to try

Local favourites

  • Gulyás: paprika-seasoned beef soup, often served with bread.
  • Lángos: fried flatbread commonly topped with sour cream and cheese.
  • Chicken paprikash: chicken in a paprika and sour-cream sauce.
  • Kürtőskalács: chimney cake, a sweet baked pastry.
  • Dobos torta: layered sponge cake with chocolate buttercream and caramel.

Dietary needs and meal budgets

Budapest has vegetarian, vegan, Indian and Middle Eastern dining options; halal availability should be checked restaurant by restaurant. Ask about ingredients, cooking fats and cross-contamination where relevant. For meal planning, allow roughly €11–€20 per day for budget meals and €37–€58 for mid-range dining; these are estimates, not menu quotations.

16 · SAFETY & PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Before you travel

Safety and local rules

  • Keep valuables secure in crowded transport, markets and busy tourist areas; pickpocketing is the common visitor concern.
  • Use licensed taxis or reputable app-based booking; agree the fare method and check the vehicle details.
  • Validate tickets as required and keep them available for inspection; fines may apply for invalid travel.
  • Carry suitable identification and follow local laws. Hungary enforces strict drug laws.
  • Check current government travel advice before regional or cross-border travel; do not rely on old advisories about neighbouring countries.

Health, payments and connectivity

  • Schengen-compliant travel medical insurance is required for a short-stay visa; check the policy wording and coverage dates.
  • Budapest tap water is generally safe to drink. Take tick precautions in wooded rural areas during warmer months.
  • Use forint for cash purchases; card payments are common. Choose bank ATMs and decline unfavourable dynamic currency conversion.
  • Local SIM/eSIM options are available; check current data packages and passport-registration requirements with the provider.
  • Emergency number: 112. Keep the Embassy of India in Budapest’s current contact details from its official website.
