01 · OVERVIEW

Hungary at a glance

Explore the country’s education and travel options with a clear understanding of language, cost, location and immigration requirements.

Country context

Hungarian universities offer English-taught options in fields such as medicine, engineering, business, IT and sciences, but availability and entry standards vary by institution and course. The University of Debrecen, for example, advertises more than 80 fully English-taught degree programmes and publishes programme-level fees. Check the official course page for the intake, entrance examination, language evidence and full fee schedule. Stipendium Hungaricum eligibility is tied to the applicant’s sending country and approved study fields; it is not a universal scholarship.

Compare cities and seasons before selecting a university or travel route. Requirements and practical conditions can differ by region.

02 · STUDY ABROAD

Academic pathways in Hungary

Shortlist by programme fit, language, total cost, recognition and student support—not reputation alone.

Study options

English-taught medicine, engineering and business routes are available. Stipendium Hungaricum is competitive for eligible partner-country applicants; benefits may not cover all living costs. Non-EU students generally need a study residence permit. Indian tourists generally need a Schengen visa. Spring and autumn are comfortable; one-stop flights via Europe/Middle East; metro, tram and rail.

  • Undergraduate: secondary-school completion and course prerequisites.
  • Postgraduate: relevant recognised bachelor’s degree; some courses require work experience.
  • Doctoral: research fit, proposal and supervisor availability.
  • Language: accepted test or local-language evidence where required.

Application checklist

  • Valid passport and application form.
  • Academic certificates and transcripts.
  • Language test or accepted medium-of-instruction evidence.
  • Statement of purpose, CV and references where required.
  • Portfolio, research proposal or interview for selected programmes.
  • Financial, medical and translated documents where requested.

Tuition & living costs

Fees differ by course, campus and student status. Obtain a current written fee breakdown including deposits, insurance, registration, examination and laboratory charges. Compare residence halls and private housing, utilities, meals and commute.

Scholarships

Stipendium Hungaricum is Hungary’s principal government scholarship scheme for eligible partner-country applicants, subject to the annual call, sending-partner nomination and programme availability. The package can include tuition coverage, a stipend, accommodation contribution and medical insurance, but the stipend may not cover all living expenses. Check the current call and country-specific sending-partner rules; university scholarships are separate.

Check the official annual call for nationality eligibility, deadlines, required documents, coverage and renewal conditions. Admission does not guarantee funding.

Student visa & admission

Indian citizens planning a full-time course lasting more than 90 days generally need a Hungarian residence permit for the purpose of studies. Apply through the competent Hungarian mission before travel unless a specific legal exception applies. The application normally requires admission, proof of language ability, accommodation, sufficient funds and health insurance. A short-stay Schengen C visa does not authorise long-term study. Follow the mission’s current appointment and document instructions.

04 · TOURISM

Plan a visit to Hungary

Hungary is a compact Central European destination, with Budapest as the main gateway and easy rail links to historic towns, wine regions and thermal-spa cities.

Season, flights & transport

Late spring and early autumn are comfortable for city walks. Summer is warm and busy; winter is cold but brings festive markets and thermal baths. From India, Budapest is usually reached with one stop through a European or Middle Eastern hub; compare total travel time, baggage and transit-visa rules. Budapest has metro, tram, bus and suburban rail; intercity trains connect major towns. As a current planning reference, BKK lists a 24-hour Budapest travelcard at HUF 2,750 and a 72-hour travelcard at HUF 5,750; recheck ticket rules and validity before purchase.

Tourist visa for Indian passport holders

Indian ordinary passport holders require a Schengen short-stay visa (Type C) for tourism in Hungary, for visits of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Apply through the competent Hungarian visa channel for your place of residence and itinerary. A visa is only permission to travel to the border; entry is decided by border authorities, who may ask for accommodation, funds, insurance and return/onward travel evidence.

Hotels & indicative budget

Budapest offers hostels, apartments and hotels across central districts; smaller cities have guesthouses and university-town accommodation. For better value, compare Districts VI, VII, VIII and IX by street and metro access rather than assuming every central address is inexpensive. In Debrecen, Szeged and Pécs, check proximity to the university and regional rail/bus stations.

Indicative visitor budget (per person)

For planning, allow roughly €55–€95 per day for a budget-to-comfortable independent trip in Budapest, including a hostel or shared-room share, meals, local transport and a modest amount of sightseeing. A private budget hotel room may be around €55–€110 per night; better-located or peak-season hotels can cost more. Outside Budapest, accommodation and daily spending can be lower. These are broad planning ranges, not live quotations; flights, Schengen visa costs, travel insurance, shopping, premium thermal baths and intercity excursions are extra.

For a 5-day trip, use approximately €275–€475 per person on the ground as an initial budget, then add flights, visa-related expenses and a contingency reserve. Check whether accommodation rates include breakfast and local taxes.

Plan for lodging, meals, public transport, museums, thermal baths, intercity rail, insurance and a contingency reserve. High season, Christmas markets and major festivals increase rates.

Attractions & entry costs

Parliament tours, museums, churches, thermal baths and castle attractions have separate ticket categories and timed-entry rules. For a concrete 2026 reference, Széchenyi Thermal Bath lists weekday daily entry with locker at HUF 13,200 and weekend entry at HUF 14,800; peak-season pricing can be higher. Parliament visitor tickets for non-EU visitors are listed from €60 on the visitor ticket site. Prices, exchange rates and eligibility can change, so confirm directly before booking.

Flexible five-day itinerary

Day 1: arrive, explore the Danube riverfront and central Pest. Day 2: Parliament area, Buda Castle district and a thermal bath. Day 3: Central Market Hall, Jewish Quarter and city museums. Day 4: choose Szentendre for an easier half-day trip, or Eger/Lake Balaton for a longer excursion. Day 5: relaxed café or market visit and departure. Check seasonal train schedules and reserve timed-entry attractions in advance.
