🎓 Study Abroad

France has universities, grandes écoles and specialist institutions across a wide range of subjects. Application routes, language expectations and tuition arrangements vary by institution and programme.

  • Bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral pathways
  • French-taught and selected English-taught programmes
  • Institution-specific admission and language requirements
  • Campus France and official institution guidance can help clarify application routes

✈️ Travel & Tourism

France offers major cultural cities, historic regions, coastal destinations, mountain scenery and world-renowned food traditions.

  • Paris and its museums and landmarks
  • French Riviera and Mediterranean coast
  • Loire Valley châteaux
  • Alps, Provence and regional heritage

Places you may wish to explore

Paris · Lyon · Nice · Bordeaux · Strasbourg · Marseille

Travel planning note: France has varied regional climates; spring and autumn are popular for many city trips, while mountain and coastal seasons differ.
