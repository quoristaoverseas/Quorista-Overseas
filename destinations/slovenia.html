🎓 Study Abroad

Slovenia is part of the European Higher Education Area and offers study at universities and other higher-education institutions. Course language, admissions and tuition arrangements vary.

  • Bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral study
  • Programmes across public and private institutions
  • English-language availability varies by course
  • Application routes and fee arrangements depend on applicant category and programme

✈️ Travel & Tourism

Slovenia offers compact travel routes between alpine lakes, mountain scenery, caves, vineyards and historic coastal and city centres.

  • Ljubljana’s old town and riverside
  • Lake Bled and the Julian Alps
  • Postojna Cave and karst landscapes
  • Piran and the Adriatic coast

Places you may wish to explore

Ljubljana · Bled · Maribor · Piran · Koper

Travel planning note: Late spring to early autumn is popular for outdoor routes; alpine weather can change quickly.
