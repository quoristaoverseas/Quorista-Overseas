🎓 Study Abroad

South Korea has universities and specialist institutions across technology, business, sciences, arts and humanities. International pathways and language expectations depend on the chosen course and institution.

  • Undergraduate and graduate programmes
  • Korean-taught and selected English-taught options
  • Language preparation may be relevant for some pathways
  • Scholarship availability and criteria vary by institution and programme

✈️ Travel & Tourism

Visitors can combine Seoul’s contemporary culture with historic palaces, coastal cities, mountain scenery and regional food traditions.

  • Seoul’s neighbourhoods, palaces and museums
  • Busan’s coastline and city attractions
  • Jeju Island’s landscapes
  • Traditional markets, cultural sites and seasonal festivals
