01 · COUNTRY OVERVIEW

South Korea at a glance

South Korea offers distinct education, career and travel possibilities. The best city and pathway depend on the subject, language, budget, season and personal circumstances.

Understanding the destination

South Korea combines research-intensive universities with applied and specialist institutions. English-medium courses are more common at graduate level and selected international colleges; many undergraduate degrees require Korean proficiency. Check the actual department-level language and TOPIK requirements.

Seoul’s palaces and neighbourhoods, Busan’s beaches and markets, Jeju’s volcanic landscapes, Gyeongju’s heritage and Korea’s food, design and pop-culture scenes offer varied itineraries.

02 · STUDY ABROAD

Build an academic pathway in South Korea

Choose a course by subject fit, language of instruction, entry requirements, total cost and career plans. Compare the exact department and campus—not only the university name.

Why consider South Korea?

  • Options across selected public, private and specialist institutions.
  • Subject pathways aligned to the country's academic and industry strengths.
  • International admissions routes at the institutions listed below.
  • Potential university, government or external scholarship opportunities.
  • Opportunity to gain international academic and cultural experience.

Education system & planning

International applicants may find bachelor's, master's, doctoral and selected foundation, language or pathway programmes. Duration and entry level vary by national framework and university.

  • Undergraduate: secondary-school completion or equivalent; subject prerequisites may apply.
  • Postgraduate: relevant recognised bachelor's degree; some courses require work experience or a portfolio.
  • Doctoral: research fit, supervisor availability, proposal and prior academic record.
  • Language: IELTS/TOEFL or local-language evidence where required; exemptions are institution-specific.
PATHWAY & VISA CATEGORIES

Language study and degree routes

Use the correct programme category and visa classification for the actual course. These are common examples, not a substitute for the university's admission notice or an immigration decision.

Korean language study

D-4-1 is commonly used for qualifying Korean-language training at eligible university-affiliated language institutes. Entry requirements, study duration, attendance and visa eligibility are institution- and applicant-specific.

Bachelor's degree

D-2-2 applies to eligible bachelor's degree study. Applicants normally need recognised secondary education and any course-specific prerequisites.

Graduate study

D-2-3 is the master's category and D-2-4 the doctoral category. Confirm degree equivalency, research fit, language requirements and department-specific criteria.

Language evidence is not universally interchangeable. IELTS, TOEFL, TOPIK, PTE Academic and Medium of Instruction letters are accepted only where the specific university and department explicitly permit them. Obtain written confirmation for the chosen intake.

Programmes & tuition

Tuition varies by institution, level, discipline and campus. The Korean government’s published national averages for degree programmes are approximately ₩6.82 million per year for undergraduate study and ₩4.22 million for graduate study. The reported undergraduate average is lower at national/public universities (about ₩4.27 million) than at private universities (about ₩7.63 million). These are national averages, not quotations for a particular course; medicine, arts, laboratory and specialist programmes may cost more.

  • Request the current fee schedule for the exact course and intake.
  • Check application, registration, examination, insurance and laboratory charges.
  • Ask whether fees are annual, semester-based or total-programme amounts.
  • Confirm refund, deposit and deferral policies in writing.

Scholarships & funding

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) is a major government-funded route with separate undergraduate and graduate calls and embassy/university tracks. The 2027 undergraduate GKS call was published in September 2026; applicants should use the current guideline, eligible-university list and deadline for their track. Universities may also offer tuition reductions based on academic performance, language scores or department nomination.

  • Check nationality and programme eligibility.
  • Confirm whether a separate scholarship application is needed.
  • Review renewal grades, attendance and duration conditions.
  • Calculate the funding gap after living costs and uncovered fees.
05 · STUDENT VISA & ADMISSION

From application to arrival

Immigration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. The university and visa route must be confirmed before paying deposits or booking travel.

Student immigration overview

International students need the appropriate status for their course. D-2 covers eligible degree-level study and includes D-2-1 (associate), D-2-2 (bachelor’s), D-2-3 (master’s), D-2-4 (doctoral), D-2-5 (research), D-2-6 (exchange) and D-2-7 (work-learning-linked study). D-4 covers qualifying non-degree training; D-4-1 is commonly used for Korean-language training at eligible institutions. Applicants generally need the university’s standard admission letter, passport, photograph, education evidence and proof of financial ability; additional documents, including a TB test where applicable, may be required. Apply through the Korean diplomatic mission responsible for your Indian residence and follow its current KVAC/VFS submission procedure.

Accommodation & living costs

The official Study in Korea portal gives a broad monthly living-cost estimate of around ₩750,000–₩1,000,000. Its guidance lists housing at roughly ₩500,000–₩700,000 per month, with dormitory fees varying by room occupancy and meal plan. Build a personal budget around the actual city, housing deposit, utilities, food, transport, insurance, phone and study materials; Seoul private housing can push costs higher.

Part-time work rights are not a substitute for proof of funds. Check the exact legal limits and permission requirements for your visa category.

06 · TOURISM & TRAVEL

Explore South Korea beyond the classroom

From Seoul’s royal palaces and lively neighbourhoods to Busan’s coast, Jeju’s volcanic scenery and Gyeongju’s historic sites, South Korea offers a mix of culture, food, shopping and nature.

Best time to visit

Spring (March–May) is popular for blossoms and mild weather; autumn (September–November) is known for comfortable temperatures and foliage. Summer (June–August) can be hot, humid and rainy, while winter (December–February) is cold, especially inland. Jeju has a milder coastal climate but remains exposed to seasonal storms.

Flights from India

Delhi has offered non-stop connections to Seoul on selected services; other Indian gateways may require a connection or have seasonal schedules. Flight duration, airline, baggage and fares change by date. Compare direct and one-stop itineraries and check whether the arrival airport is Incheon (ICN) or Gimpo (GMP) before booking.

Visitor visa for Indian passport holders

Indian ordinary-passport holders should obtain the appropriate Korean visitor visa before travel unless a specific exemption applies to their exact itinerary. Do not assume K-ETA is available as a substitute for a visa. The Korean mission's jurisdiction is determined by the applicant's residence/passport-issue details and current India submission arrangements. The northern region is served by the Embassy in New Delhi, central India by the Consulate in Mumbai, and southern India by the Consulate in Chennai; designated KVACs or agencies handle submissions depending on jurisdiction. Always download the latest C-3 checklist and fee instructions before applying.

Local transport & useful apps

Major cities have extensive metro and bus networks. A T-money or compatible transit card is useful for local journeys; reload options and card acceptance can vary. KTX and other intercity trains connect major cities, but book popular routes early around holidays. Google Maps has limited turn-by-turn navigation functionality in South Korea; install Naver Map or KakaoMap, Kakao T for taxis and Papago for translation before arrival. Check live service notices and route details.

Accommodation planning

Choose a hotel or guesthouse close to the stations and neighbourhoods you plan to visit. Myeongdong, Hongdae and Seoul Station are useful Seoul bases; Haeundae, Seomyeon and Busan Station serve different Busan itineraries. In Jeju, accommodation choice depends on whether you plan to rent a car or use buses and tours.

Indicative nightly planning bands (not live quotes):

  • Hostel / guesthouse: ₩35,000–₩65,000 per room/bed option, depending on privacy and season.
  • 3-star / business hotel: ₩95,000–₩145,000 per room.
  • 4-star hotel: ₩165,000–₩260,000 per room.
  • Premium / luxury: ₩380,000 and above per room.

Rates vary significantly by city, dates, room occupancy, cancellation policy and event periods. Confirm taxes, breakfast, deposits, check-in times and the final amount with the property before payment.

Food & dining

Try bibimbap, kimchi jjigae, bulgogi, Korean barbecue, tteokbokki, hotteok and market snacks. A simple street-food item may cost around ₩3,000–₩6,000, casual meals around ₩8,000–₩13,000 and Korean barbecue around ₩16,000–₩35,000 per person, depending on location and restaurant. Vegetarian, vegan and halal options exist, but check ingredients, broths, sauces and preparation practices directly with the restaurant.

07 · PLACES & EXPERIENCES

Places worth adding to your itinerary

Entry fees, opening days, timed-entry rules and seasonal access change. Check attraction operators and official tourism sources close to your visit.

CAPITAL & CULTURE

COASTAL CITY

Busan

HISTORIC HERITAGE

Bulguksa Temple, Seokguram Grotto, Daereungwon tomb complex and Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond.

VOLCANIC ISLAND

Jeju

Seongsan Ilchulbong, Hallasan trails, waterfalls, lava landscapes and coastal viewpoints. Weather can affect access.

Getting around: indicative fares

  • Metro / city bus: a typical base fare is around ₩1,400–₩1,500; route, distance, transfer and city affect the fare.
  • T-money card: card purchase is separate from travel credit; keep some cash available for purchase or top-ups where needed.
  • AREX airport express: Incheon Airport–Seoul Station express service is about 43 minutes; check current fare and timetable.
  • KTX Seoul–Busan: standard one-way adult fare is often around ₩59,800, with timetable and fare subject to change.
  • Taxi: metered fares vary by city, distance, time and surcharges; use licensed taxis and confirm destination in a map app.

These are planning references, not guaranteed prices. Check operator sites and ticketing apps before travel.

  • Mid-range: approximately ₩140,000–₩220,000.

08 · SAMPLE ITINERARY

7 days in South Korea: Seoul, Gyeongju & Busan

A sample route for a first visit. It assumes arrival and departure through Seoul/Incheon and should be adjusted to flight times, rail availability and traveller interests.

DAY 1 · SEOUL

Arrival & Myeongdong

Airport transfer, hotel check-in, a relaxed neighbourhood walk and street-food exploration.

DAY 2 · SEOUL

Palaces & old neighbourhoods

Gyeongbokgung, Bukchon, Insadong and a city-view visit around sunset.

DAY 3 · SEOUL

Modern Seoul

Choose a museum, Hangang riverside experience, Hongdae or Gangnam; book any special attraction in advance.

DAY 4 · BUSAN

Travel by KTX

Take a morning train to Busan, check in and explore the coast or Haeundae area.

DAY 5 · GYEONGJU

Heritage excursion

Visit Gyeongju’s historic sites on a day trip, allowing time for the return journey to Busan.

DAY 6 · BUSAN → SEOUL

Coastal city & return

Explore Gamcheon or Jagalchi, then return to Seoul by a pre-booked train if required by the flight schedule.

DAY 7 · DEPARTURE

Airport transfer

Allow sufficient time for check-out, airport travel, airline check-in and immigration formalities.

Optional Jeju extension: Add 2 nights or more and plan a separate domestic flight, accommodation and local transport. Jeju is not a practical add-on to every 7-day Seoul–Busan itinerary. Check weather, driving eligibility, insurance and seasonal attraction access before confirming.
Travel safety & practical notes: Emergency numbers are 112 (police) and 119 (fire/ambulance); 1330 is the Korea Travel Helpline. Keep travel insurance and emergency contacts accessible, use licensed transport, follow local laws, check weather and official advisories, and keep some cash for transit-card top-ups and small vendors. Carry suitable identification and secure valuables in crowded areas.
