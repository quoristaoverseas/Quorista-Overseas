01 · COUNTRY OVERVIEW

South Korea at a glance

South Korea offers distinct education, career and travel possibilities. The best city and pathway depend on the subject, language, budget, season and personal circumstances.

Understanding the destination

South Korea combines research-intensive universities with applied and specialist institutions. English-medium courses are more common at graduate level and selected international colleges; many undergraduate degrees require Korean proficiency. Check the actual department-level language and TOPIK requirements.

Seoul’s palaces and neighbourhoods, Busan’s beaches and markets, Jeju’s volcanic landscapes, Gyeongju’s heritage and Korea’s food, design and pop-culture scenes offer varied itineraries.

02 · STUDY ABROAD

Build an academic pathway in South Korea

Choose a course by subject fit, language of instruction, entry requirements, total cost and career plans. Compare the exact department and campus—not only the university name.

Why consider South Korea?

  • Options across selected public, private and specialist institutions.
  • Subject pathways aligned to the country's academic and industry strengths.
  • International admissions routes at the institutions listed below.
  • Potential university, government or external scholarship opportunities.
  • Opportunity to gain international academic and cultural experience.

Education system & planning

International applicants may find bachelor's, master's, doctoral and selected foundation, language or pathway programmes. Duration and entry level vary by national framework and university.

  • Undergraduate: secondary-school completion or equivalent; subject prerequisites may apply.
  • Postgraduate: relevant recognised bachelor's degree; some courses require work experience or a portfolio.
  • Doctoral: research fit, supervisor availability, proposal and prior academic record.
  • Language: IELTS/TOEFL or local-language evidence where required; exemptions are institution-specific.

Programmes & tuition

Tuition varies by institution, level, discipline and campus. The Korean government’s published national averages for degree programmes are approximately ₩6.82 million per year for undergraduate study and ₩4.22 million for graduate study. The reported undergraduate average is lower at national/public universities (about ₩4.27 million) than at private universities (about ₩7.63 million). These are national averages, not quotations for a particular course; medicine, arts, laboratory and specialist programmes may cost more.

  • Request the current fee schedule for the exact course and intake.
  • Check application, registration, examination, insurance and laboratory charges.
  • Ask whether fees are annual, semester-based or total-programme amounts.
  • Confirm refund, deposit and deferral policies in writing.

Scholarships & funding

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) is a major government-funded route with separate undergraduate and graduate calls and embassy/university tracks. The 2027 undergraduate GKS call was published in September 2026; applicants should use the current guideline, eligible-university list and deadline for their track. Universities may also offer tuition reductions based on academic performance, language scores or department nomination.

  • Check nationality and programme eligibility.
  • Confirm whether a separate scholarship application is needed.
  • Review renewal grades, attendance and duration conditions.
  • Calculate the funding gap after living costs and uncovered fees.
05 · STUDENT VISA & ADMISSION

From application to arrival

Immigration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. The university and visa route must be confirmed before paying deposits or booking travel.

Student immigration overview

International students need the appropriate status for their course: D-2 for degree programmes (associate, bachelor’s, master’s, doctoral and exchange) and D-4 for qualifying non-degree training such as Korean-language study. Applicants generally need the university’s standard admission letter, passport, photograph, education evidence and proof of financial ability; additional documents, including a TB test where applicable, may be required. Apply through the Korean diplomatic mission responsible for your Indian residence and follow its current KVAC/VFS submission procedure.

Accommodation & living costs

The official Study in Korea portal gives a broad monthly living-cost estimate of around ₩750,000–₩1,000,000. Its guidance lists housing at roughly ₩500,000–₩700,000 per month, with dormitory fees varying by room occupancy and meal plan. Build a personal budget around the actual city, housing deposit, utilities, food, transport, insurance, phone and study materials; Seoul private housing can push costs higher.

Part-time work rights are not a substitute for proof of funds. Check the exact legal limits and permission requirements for your visa category.

06 · TOURISM & TRAVEL

Explore South Korea beyond the classroom

Seoul’s palaces and neighbourhoods, Busan’s beaches and markets, Jeju’s volcanic landscapes, Gyeongju’s heritage and Korea’s food, design and pop-culture scenes offer varied itineraries.

Best time to visit

Spring (April–May) and autumn (September–November) are popular for comfortable weather. Summer can be hot, humid and rainy; winter is cold, especially inland.

Flights from India

Direct flights operate from major Indian gateways to Seoul on selected airlines; one-stop connections may widen options. Check current schedules and entry airport before purchasing.

Getting around

Seoul and other major cities have extensive metro and bus networks. A rechargeable T-money or compatible transit card is useful for local journeys; intercity rail, including KTX, connects major destinations. Check the correct rail station, seat reservations and luggage arrangements before travelling, and allow extra time for transfers.

Visitor visa for Indian passport holders

Indian ordinary passport holders generally need the appropriate Korean visitor visa before travelling for tourism. Do not assume K-ETA is a substitute where a visa is required. Confirm the current visa category, checklist and submission route with the Korean Visa Portal and the Korean mission responsible for your state of residence. In India, jurisdiction is divided between the Embassy in New Delhi and the Consulates in Mumbai and Chennai.

Hotels & accommodation

Choose accommodation by neighbourhood and transport access, not only nightly price. Compare cancellation terms, taxes, breakfast, airport transfer, family-room configuration and late check-in. In peak periods, reserve refundable options early.

Transport, tickets & entry fees

Use official attraction websites or authorised ticket sellers for current admission prices, timed-entry reservations and closure notices. Budget for local transit, intercity travel, airport transfers and guided excursions separately. Keep digital and offline copies of tickets and accommodation details.

Indicative travel budget

Indicative daily spend: a Seoul backpacker-style day may be planned around ₩60,000–90,000 per person, a comfortable mid-range day around ₩120,000–180,000, and a higher-comfort day around ₩220,000–300,000, including accommodation, food, city transport and ordinary sightseeing. Busan and some regional cities can be less expensive; Jeju may cost more when car hire or tours are needed.

For travellers departing India, a 5–7 day mid-range trip has been estimated at approximately ₹1.3–2.2 lakh per person including return flights, visa and on-ground costs.
