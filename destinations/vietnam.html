🎓 Study Abroad

Vietnam’s higher-education sector includes national universities, specialist institutions and international collaborations. English-language offerings and international admissions vary by institution.

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate programmes
  • Options across business, technology, sciences and other fields
  • Programme-specific language and academic criteria
  • Confirm recognition, delivery language and admissions directly with the institution

✈️ Travel & Tourism

Vietnam’s varied geography supports city breaks, heritage travel, mountain routes and beach holidays.

  • Hanoi’s Old Quarter and cultural landmarks
  • Ha Long Bay and northern landscapes
  • Hoi An and central Vietnam heritage
  • Ho Chi Minh City and Mekong Delta experiences
