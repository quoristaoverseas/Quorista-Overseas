🎓 Study Abroad

Singapore’s higher-education ecosystem includes universities and specialised institutions with internationally oriented programmes. Entry requirements and selection are competitive and programme-specific.

  • Universities and specialised education institutions
  • Undergraduate, postgraduate and professional programmes
  • Academic results and programme prerequisites are important
  • Student’s Pass and other immigration matters must be checked against current official rules

✈️ Travel & Tourism

Singapore is well suited to a city-focused visit, with waterfront attractions, gardens, cultural districts, shopping and a celebrated food scene.

  • Marina Bay and the city skyline
  • Gardens by the Bay and green spaces
  • Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Gelam
  • Sentosa and family attractions

Places you may wish to explore

Marina Bay · Orchard · Chinatown · Sentosa · Jurong

Travel planning note: Singapore is warm and humid year-round; rainfall patterns and outdoor plans should be considered when choosing dates.
