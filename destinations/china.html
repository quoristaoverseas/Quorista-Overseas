🎓 Study Abroad

China offers a broad higher-education landscape spanning science, engineering, medicine, business, humanities and language study. Programme language, admissions and funding options differ by institution.

  • Bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral pathways
  • Programmes taught in Chinese and, at selected institutions, English
  • University-specific academic and language requirements
  • Scholarship opportunities with separate eligibility and application rules

✈️ Travel & Tourism

China’s scale creates very different travel experiences, from imperial heritage and historic towns to contemporary skylines and dramatic natural landscapes.

  • Beijing and the Great Wall region
  • Shanghai and modern city culture
  • Xi’an and historic heritage
  • Chengdu, Guilin and other nature and culture routes

Beijing · Shanghai · Xi’an · Chengdu · Guangzhou
