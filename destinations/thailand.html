🎓 Study Abroad

Thailand has public and private universities offering programmes across business, technology, health, hospitality and other fields. International programme options and entry conditions should be checked directly with each institution.

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate options
  • Selected international programmes at universities
  • Course-specific academic and language requirements
  • Study-related immigration permission is distinct from a tourist visit

✈️ Travel & Tourism

Thailand offers a mix of energetic cities, historic sites, northern landscapes and island destinations.

  • Bangkok’s temples, markets and city life
  • Chiang Mai and northern cultural experiences
  • Phuket, Krabi and island coastlines
  • Ayutthaya and other historic sites
