01 · COUNTRY OVERVIEW

United Arab Emirates at a glance

United Arab Emirates offers distinct education, career and travel possibilities. The best city and pathway depend on the subject, language, budget, season and personal circumstances.

Understanding the destination

The UAE is a regional higher-education hub with federal universities, private institutions and international branch campuses. English-taught degrees are common, particularly in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Check campus accreditation, programme accreditation and whether the qualification meets requirements in the country where you intend to work.

Dubai’s skyline and desert experiences, Abu Dhabi’s cultural district and mosques, Sharjah’s museums, Ras Al Khaimah’s mountains and Fujairah’s coastline make the Emirates suitable for city breaks and family travel.

02 · STUDY ABROAD

Build an academic pathway in United Arab Emirates

Shortlist by programme fit and real entry requirements—not reputation alone. Confirm the exact campus, awarding institution, teaching language and professional recognition.

Why consider United Arab Emirates?

  • Options across selected public, private and specialist institutions.
  • Subject pathways aligned to the country's academic and industry strengths.
  • International admissions routes at the institutions listed below.
  • Potential university, government or external scholarship opportunities.
  • Opportunity to gain international academic and cultural experience.

Education system & planning

International applicants may find bachelor's, master's, doctoral and selected foundation, language or pathway programmes. Duration and entry level vary by national framework and university.

  • Undergraduate: secondary-school completion or equivalent; subject prerequisites may apply.
  • Postgraduate: relevant recognised bachelor's degree; some courses require work experience or a portfolio.
  • Doctoral: research fit, supervisor availability, proposal and prior academic record.
  • Language: IELTS/TOEFL or local-language evidence where required; exemptions are institution-specific.

Programmes & tuition

Tuition varies by institution, level, discipline, language, campus and student nationality. Medicine, laboratory-based courses, business schools and private institutions may have substantially different fee structures.

  • Request the current fee schedule for the exact course and intake.
  • Check application, registration, examination, insurance and laboratory charges.
  • Ask whether fees are annual, semester-based or total-programme amounts.
  • Confirm refund, deposit and deferral policies in writing.

Scholarships & funding

Institutional merit awards, need-based assistance and selected government scholarship schemes exist. Eligibility and award size differ by university, nationality, programme and intake. Secure the exact written award conditions before budgeting.

  • Check nationality and programme eligibility.
  • Confirm whether a separate scholarship application is needed.
  • Review renewal grades, attendance and duration conditions.
  • Calculate the funding gap after living costs and uncovered fees.
05 · STUDENT VISA & ADMISSION

From application to arrival

Immigration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. The university and visa route must be confirmed before paying deposits or booking travel.

Student immigration overview

International students usually obtain a student residence visa through an accredited university or an eligible parent sponsor. The university normally guides the application after admission. Requirements may include a valid passport, admission/enrolment evidence, medical fitness screening, Emirates ID/residence formalities and health insurance, depending on the emirate and sponsor. A student residence visa is separate from a tourist/visit visa; confirm the sponsor, validity, renewal and cancellation conditions with the university before paying deposits.

Compare university residences, private student housing, shared apartments and homestays. Ask about deposits, utilities, meal plans, commute times and contract length. Living-cost estimates should be built city by city and include insurance, local transport, phone, study materials and emergency funds.

Part-time work rights are not a substitute for proof of funds. Check the exact legal limits and permission requirements for your visa category.

06 · TOURISM & TRAVEL

Explore United Arab Emirates beyond the classroom

Dubai’s skyline and desert experiences, Abu Dhabi’s cultural district and mosques, Sharjah’s museums, Ras Al Khaimah’s mountains and Fujairah’s coastline make the Emirates suitable for city breaks and family travel.

Best time to visit

November–March is generally the most comfortable outdoor season. Summer is extremely hot; plan indoor attractions and check Ramadan opening hours and public-holiday schedules.

Flights from India

Frequent direct and one-stop services connect major Indian cities with Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Compare airport, baggage, transit conditions and arrival time; routes vary by season and airline.

Getting around

Indian ordinary passport holders should not assume visa-free entry to the UAE. A limited 14-day visa on arrival may be available to Indian citizens holding a qualifying valid US visa/Green Card or UK/EU residence visa (with required validity); other qualifying residence permits may be covered under the federal ICP route. Travellers who do not meet the conditions generally need a pre-arranged visit visa through an authorised airline, hotel, travel agency or UAE sponsor. Passport validity and supporting-document conditions apply.

Hotels & accommodation

Choose accommodation by neighbourhood and transport access, not only nightly price. Compare cancellation terms, taxes, breakfast, airport transfer, family-room configuration and late check-in. In peak periods, reserve refundable options early.

Transport, tickets & entry fees

Use official attraction websites or authorised ticket sellers for current admission prices, timed-entry reservations and closure notices. Budget for local transit, intercity travel, airport transfers and guided excursions separately. Keep digital and offline copies of tickets and accommodation details.

Indicative travel budget

Budget varies sharply by emirate, hotel category, season and transport choices. Dubai and Abu Dhabi peak-season stays can be expensive; compare hotel taxes, breakfast, transfers and attraction bundles.

Build a daily budget covering hotel, meals, local transport, attraction tickets, intercity transfers, insurance, SIM/data and contingency. Prices shown by third-party sites can exclude taxes or service charges.
