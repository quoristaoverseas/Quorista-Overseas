  • Undergraduate: secondary school completion and subject prerequisites.
  • Postgraduate: relevant recognised bachelor’s degree; some courses require experience or portfolio.
  • Doctoral: research fit, proposal and supervisor availability.
  • Language: accepted test or local-language certificate where required.

Application documents & living

  • Passport, application form, certificates and transcripts.
  • Language evidence, statement of purpose and CV.
  • References, portfolio or research proposal where required.
  • Financial, medical, insurance and translated documents as specified.

Most public universities do not charge general tuition for many consecutive bachelor’s and master’s programmes, but students still pay a semester contribution. Important exceptions include non-EU tuition in Baden-Württemberg (generally €1,500 per semester) and fees set by some Bavarian universities; private and continuing-education programmes may cost much more. DAAD estimates living costs around €900–€1,200 monthly, while the 2026 study-visa financial proof is €11,904 for a year. Budget separately for deposits, insurance, travel and semester charges.

Check nationality eligibility, deadlines, coverage, renewal grades and service obligations. Admission does not guarantee a scholarship.

Student visa & admission

Applicants who need a visa should apply for the appropriate national visa for study before travelling. A state-recognised university admission is normally required; proof of funds, health insurance and language evidence may also be needed. The official 2026 living-cost proof reference is €11,904 for a year (commonly via a blocked account, scholarship or accepted commitment). After arrival, complete the local residence-permit formalities. Requirements and application routing for Indian residents depend on course and category; use the German mission’s current checklist.

Best season, flights & transport

May to September is generally favourable for longer days, outdoor activities and city touring; Christmas markets and winter sports make December to February attractive for a different trip. Direct flights from India commonly serve Frankfurt and Munich, with other gateways depending on airline and season. Germany’s long-distance trains connect major cities, while regional trains, S-Bahn, U-Bahn, trams and buses serve local journeys. Compare train fares and book popular long-distance routes early; allow extra transfer time.

Check weather and flight schedules for your exact dates. Compare baggage, arrival airports, transit visa needs and onward transfers. Use licensed local transport.

Tourist visa for Indian passport holders

Indian ordinary passport holders generally require a Schengen short-stay visa for tourism in Germany, normally for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. Apply through the competent German mission/authorised service channel for your place of residence and main destination. Carry evidence of itinerary, accommodation, travel medical insurance, sufficient funds and return/onward travel. Check current fees, appointment routing and document rules before applying.

Hotels & budgets

Accommodation varies substantially by city and event calendar. In Berlin, compare well-connected districts beyond the immediate tourist core; in Munich, book early for trade fairs and Oktoberfest; in Hamburg and Heidelberg, compare station access with neighbourhood character. Hostels and guesthouses can lower costs, while apartment stays may add cleaning fees and city taxes. Check whether the room rate includes breakfast and local visitor taxes.

Indicative visitor budget (per person)

For a budget-to-mid-range trip, plan roughly €80–€150 per day, including an accommodation share, meals, local transport and ordinary sightseeing. A basic private hotel room may be around €70–€150 per night in many cities, but Munich, major fairs, Christmas markets and peak weekends can be considerably higher. Hostels and advance-booked rooms may cost less.

For 5 days, use about €400–€750 per person on the ground as a starting estimate. Return flights from India, Schengen visa expenses, insurance, long-distance rail, car hire and premium attractions are additional. Book long-distance trains early and compare the full fare, including seat reservations and local transfers.

Germany's city-to-city costs differ; Munich and major event dates can exceed these ranges, while smaller university towns may be more economical. Treat the figures as a planning baseline, not a quotation.

Flexible five-day itinerary

Day 1: arrive in Berlin and explore the central historic district. Day 2: choose the Museum Island area or a guided history visit; check timed-entry availability. Day 3: take a relaxed neighbourhood/food day or visit Potsdam. Day 4: use a regional excursion only if rail time suits your plans. Day 5: market, park or museum visit and departure. This is a Berlin-based example; Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt and the Rhine region need separate routing because Germany is too large for several distant cities in five days.

Safety: Monitor official travel advisories and weather alerts, respect local laws and cultural norms, keep emergency contacts and insurance details accessible, and check activity-operator safety standards.

Current entry and study-permit checks

Official-source check for planning purposes. Reconfirm the rules for the traveller’s nationality, exact purpose and intended travel date before payment or departure.

Germany: use the 2026 financial and work rules. For a German study visa in 2026, the official federal information portal states that applicants can demonstrate living-cost funding through a blocked account of at least €11,904, a scholarship or a declaration of commitment. This is proof of funds, not the total cost of a degree or a guaranteed living-cost budget for every city. Under the current study residence rules, students may work up to 140 full days or 280 half days per year, or up to 20 hours per week. Indian ordinary passport holders need a visa for tourism; short visits use the Schengen C-visa route, while degree study normally requires the relevant national visa and subsequent residence formalities.
