Scholarships Indian Students Can Still Apply For: Deadlines from December 2026 to March 2027

A practical, date-checked shortlist for Indian students planning 2027 entry. It separates applications that are genuinely open from stages that have closed and scholarship calls that have not yet been announced.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 14 min readPublished: 26 November 2026Reviewed: 26 November 2026

For a broader explanation of how to compare scholarship coverage, funding gaps and application routes, see our complete guide to fully funded scholarships abroad and the dedicated Commonwealth scholarship route comparison for Indian applicants.

Scholarship deadlines do not all fall on one calendar. Some programmes require a central scholarship application and a separate university application; others add a nomination stage run by the applicant's home country. A student can therefore miss an opportunity even after completing one of the portals.

This guide is a dated snapshot for Indian applicants planning study beginning in 2027. It focuses on opportunities with a verifiable official status as of 26 November 2026. Where a call has not been published, the article says so rather than inventing an opening date.

Urgent: The Commonwealth Shared Scholarship 2027/28 deadline is 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT (21:30 India time). The separate India-nominated Commonwealth Master's route has a SAKSHAT deadline of 30 November 2026, but its CSC Central application stage closed on 20 October. SAKSHAT alone cannot replace the missing CSC application.

1. Commonwealth Shared Scholarship 2027/28

This is the clearest live opportunity in this shortlist. The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC) states that applications for the 2027/28 academic year are open and close at 16:00 GMT on Tuesday 8 December 2026. The awards support full-time, normally one-year taught Master's courses in the UK beginning in September/October 2027. They are not undergraduate or PhD scholarships.

Who should investigate it?

  • You are a citizen of, and permanently resident in, an eligible Commonwealth country. India is included in the relevant eligible-country group.
  • By September 2027, you will hold a first degree at least equivalent to an upper second-class (2:1) honours degree; a lower second-class degree may be considered with a relevant postgraduate qualification, usually a Master's.
  • You have not studied or worked for more than one academic year in a high-income country.
  • You would not otherwise be able to afford the UK course without the scholarship.
  • Your intended course appears on the CSC's approved 2027/28 course list at a participating university.

These are CSC-level criteria, not a substitute for the course's own admission requirements. A university can have additional academic, subject, English-language or application requirements.

How the application works

  1. Open the CSC's 2027/28 approved-course list and identify courses that genuinely match your previous education and development goals.
  2. Read the course page on the university website. Check its admission requirements, English-language evidence, application deadline and whether a separate university application is required immediately.
  3. Prepare the CSC Central application using the current guidance and required supporting documents.
  4. Submit the CSC application by 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026. The CSC says it cannot accept applications or documents sent by email or post instead of the online system.
  5. Apply separately for admission to the university. Scholarship application does not automatically create an admission application or guarantee an offer.

Applicants may apply for more than one course and university under the CSC rules, but may accept only one Commonwealth Shared Scholarship. Check the current application system and university instructions for the exact permitted choices.

What funding should you expect?

The award is jointly funded by the CSC and participating universities. The published scheme includes approved return airfare, tuition fees, a living allowance and other support described in the current CSC terms. Read the current award page and the participating university's own explanation before budgeting; do not assume dependent travel, family living costs, optional course costs or every personal expense will be covered.

Deadline conversion: 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026 is 21:30 Indian Standard Time on the same date. Submit earlier. Do not plan to upload references, transcripts or a final essay in the last few minutes.

2. India's Commonwealth Master's nomination route: understand the two portals

The Government of India's Ministry of Education has a separate 2027 Commonwealth Master's nomination process. It is not the same as the Commonwealth Shared Scholarship. The Ministry's notice requires an application to the CSC Central portal and a separate application to the SAKSHAT portal.

Critical status: the CSC Central window for this India-nominated award ran from 8 September to 20 October 2026 and is closed. The SAKSHAT portal is scheduled to remain open from 30 October to 30 November 2026. Therefore, the remaining SAKSHAT stage is relevant only to applicants who already completed the CSC stage and can provide the submitted CSC application PDF.

Key Indian eligibility points in the Ministry notice

  • Indian citizenship and permanent residence in India.
  • Availability to begin study in the UK at the start of the September 2027 academic year.
  • At least 60% in graduation (the Ministry's stated Indian equivalent of a 2:1), or 50–59% plus a relevant postgraduate Master's qualification for the stated 2:2 route.
  • One-year taught Master's courses only; MBA programmes are not funded under this call.
  • No age limit is stated in the Ministry notice.
  • Full higher-education transcripts, including current-to-date transcripts, and at least two references.

The Ministry notice states that 39 nominations will be made to the CSC. A Ministry nomination is not the final scholarship award; final selection rests with the CSC. It also sets a specific requirement for a confirmed UK university offer by 16:00 on 30 November 2026 for candidates to be considered under the nomination criteria. Read the full notice, including the scoring factors and return-to-India commitment scoring, before deciding whether your application is viable.

If you did not submit the CSC Central application by 20 October, do not submit SAKSHAT as though it can reopen that stage. Contact the Ministry only if you need clarification about an application you actually submitted; do not rely on informal assurances that a missing stage can be waived.

3. GKS Graduate 2027: prepare, but wait for the call

As of 26 November 2026, the official Study in Korea notice located for graduate recruitment is the 2026 GKS Graduate (GKS-G) guideline, published on 2 February 2026. The 2027 undergraduate call has been published, but that is a different programme and does not establish the 2027 graduate deadline, quota or document rules.

Applicants considering a Master's or PhD in Korea can prepare a profile now, but should not treat the 2026 graduate PDF as the final 2027 rulebook. Monitor the NIIED Study in Korea scholarship notice board and the Korean Embassy / Korean Education Centre in India for the 2027 GKS-G announcement.

Preparation that remains useful

  • Check the general age and degree-level framework on NIIED's GKS page, then recheck the exact date and exceptions in the new annual guide.
  • Build a university and department shortlist using the current university information files only as a research starting point. The 2027 list may change.
  • Prepare a clear study plan, personal statement, recommendation strategy and research proposal if your intended programme requires one.
  • Collect transcripts, degree certificates, passport and family-nationality documents. Wait for the annual guide before paying for apostilles, notarisation or translations that may not be required in your exact route.
  • Check whether your intended department is taught in English or Korean, and whether Korean-language training or TOPIK evidence is required for that category.

GKS commonly distinguishes Embassy Track and University Track. The Embassy Track begins through the Korean diplomatic mission and proceeds through national selection stages; the University Track begins with a designated university. The permitted categories, quotas, application method, university choices and deadlines are annual-call matters. Do not choose a route based on a social-media claim that one track is automatically easier.

GKS benefits can include airfare, Korean-language training, tuition and monthly allowances. The actual amounts, eligibility, duration and exceptions must be checked in the 2027 graduate guideline and award terms. A scholarship award does not remove the need to complete the applicable university and immigration procedures.

4. Stipendium Hungaricum 2027/28: call opening soon

On 26 November 2026, the official Stipendium Hungaricum website published an announcement that applications for the 2027/28 programme will open soon. The detailed call and exact deadline were not included in that announcement. It is therefore useful to prepare now, but not responsible to publish an assumed closing date.

India is listed as a sending partner, with the University Grants Commission (UGC) identified as the responsible authority. The currently published India partner page lists full-degree Bachelor's, Master's and doctoral study primarily in natural and life sciences, information technology, economics, business and management, and engineering. It lists one-tier Master's as unavailable. These are the currently visible partner fields; check the 2027/28 call and India-specific partner instructions for any changes.

How the selection route works

The programme is based on bilateral education agreements. Applicants select only study types and fields supported by the agreement with their sending partner. The official programme FAQ explains that applicants must apply through the online Tempus Public Foundation system and be nominated by the sending partner; they do not simply apply directly to a university for a Stipendium award. University admission examinations or assessment normally follow after the application stage.

For India, monitor both the Tempus Public Foundation's official call and UGC's instructions. Do not assume that the global deadline automatically tells you whether UGC requires a separate form, additional documents or a separate submission date. Follow both sets of instructions as soon as they are published.

Funding: generous support, but not a complete living-cost guarantee

The programme's currently published benefits for Bachelor's and Master's level include tuition-free education, a monthly stipend of HUF 43,700, a dormitory place or HUF 40,000 per month accommodation contribution, and medical insurance under the stated conditions. The official site explicitly warns that the stipend and housing contribution are contributions to living expenses and may not cover the full cost, particularly in Budapest. Doctoral benefits use a different stipend structure. These are currently published programme figures, not a promise that the 2027/28 call will leave every amount unchanged.

Budget separately for deposits, travel, visa/residence formalities, food, transport, winter clothing, books, and any rent above the housing contribution. Do not accept an offer on the assumption that the stipend alone will meet every monthly expense.

6. Documents worth preparing now

  • Passport with sufficient validity and consistent personal details.
  • All academic transcripts, degree certificates and grading-scale explanations.
  • Current-to-date transcripts and expected-graduation letters if still studying.
  • CV with dates, responsibilities, research, projects and relevant employment.
  • Two or more potential referees who can write specific, evidence-based recommendations.
  • Course-specific statement of purpose, study plan or research proposal.
  • English or other language test results where required by the chosen course.
  • Evidence of employment, research, leadership or community work where relevant.

Keep high-quality scans and a document tracker. Do not order every possible notarisation, apostille or translation in advance: the required format depends on the scholarship, university and application stage.

7. Avoid deadline and scholarship scams

  • Do not pay an agent who promises a guaranteed government scholarship or a guaranteed nomination.
  • Use official government, scholarship and university domains for deadlines and forms.
  • Never share portal passwords, one-time codes or full account access with an intermediary.
  • Check whether a “scholarship offer” is a real award letter or only a university tuition discount.
  • Do not treat an old cycle's deadline, stipend or eligible-university list as current.
  • Save the submitted application, confirmation email and official notice PDF.

FAQs

Can I still apply for the India-nominated Commonwealth Master's scholarship if I missed CSC Central?

The CSC Central stage closed on 20 October 2026. The remaining SAKSHAT window does not replace it. The Ministry notice requires both stages.

Is Commonwealth Shared the same as the Indian Ministry-nominated Commonwealth scholarship?

No. They are separate routes with different application processes. Commonwealth Shared is applied for through CSC Central and participating UK universities; the India-nominated route adds the Ministry's SAKSHAT process and nomination rules.

Is the GKS Graduate 2027 application open?

The 2027 graduate guideline was not published on the official notice board as of 26 November 2026. The 2026 guide is a preparation reference only.

Has Stipendium Hungaricum 2027/28 opened?

The programme announced on 26 November that applications will open soon, but the detailed call and exact deadline were not in that announcement.

Does a fully funded scholarship mean I need no personal money?

No. Check when payments start, what travel and accommodation costs are excluded, and whether the stipend is enough for the actual city and your circumstances.
