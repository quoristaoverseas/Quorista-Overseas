MEXT Scholarship Japan: A Complete Guide for Indian Students

Japan's MEXT scholarship supports selected international students across several academic categories. Learn how Indian applicants can identify the right route and prepare for the official selection process.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 12 min readUpdated: 25 November 2026Reviewed: 25 November 2026

The Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship is funded by Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology. It offers opportunities for international students and researchers across different academic categories. For Indian applicants, recruitment information is published through the Embassy of Japan in India and, for some routes, through Japanese universities.

MEXT is not one single application for every student. Categories differ by academic level, purpose, age, field, duration and selection method. The first step is to identify the correct category—such as undergraduate, research student, teacher training, Japanese studies, college of technology or specialised training college—rather than preparing a generic application.

Current-cycle note: The Embassy of Japan in India published 2027 recruitment information in 2026, and its page indicated that listed 2027 application periods had closed. Applicants planning a later intake should wait for the next official call and use the new guidelines.

1. What is the MEXT Scholarship?

MEXT scholarships aim to attract talented international students to Japan, support academic exchange and strengthen educational and research links. The scholarship is administered under different categories with distinct recruitment schedules and requirements.

Some categories are designed for students entering a full degree; others begin as research students who may later sit for entrance examinations or progress to a master's or doctoral course. The term “research student” should not be mistaken for automatic admission to a degree programme.

2. Main scholarship categories

Undergraduate Students

This category supports eligible students pursuing undergraduate education in Japan. The application guidelines specify subject groups, preparatory education arrangements, examinations and the expected period of study. Applicants should check the subject restrictions carefully, particularly where they are choosing a broad field such as humanities, social sciences or natural sciences.

Research Students

This category is relevant to graduates seeking research at a Japanese university, often with a pathway to master's or doctoral study subject to university admission and examination requirements. A clear research proposal and appropriate academic background are important. Applicants should identify research areas and potential academic fit rather than choosing Japan solely because funding is available.

Other specialised categories

MEXT may recruit teacher training students, Japanese studies students, college of technology students and specialised training college students under separate guidelines. Not all categories are open in every recruitment cycle or to every nationality in the same way. The Embassy of Japan in India publishes the current India-specific categories and application details.

3. What does MEXT funding cover?

MEXT benefits generally include a monthly scholarship allowance, exemption from entrance examination, matriculation and tuition fees at the host institution, and travel support under the conditions stated in the relevant guidelines. The amount and precise travel arrangements depend on the category and current rules.

Applicants should read the benefit section carefully. Check the monthly allowance, whether the first payment arrives after arrival and enrolment, travel route restrictions, airport requirements and any conditions around the return ticket. Personal expenses, family travel, accommodation deposits and costs outside the scholarship terms may still require separate funds.

4. Eligibility for Indian students

Eligibility varies substantially by category. The current call may specify date-of-birth ranges, previous education, subject area, academic performance, language ability and other requirements. For example, undergraduate applicants must meet the required school education and age conditions, while research applicants must have the appropriate university qualification and a research plan relevant to their intended field.

Use the exact date-of-birth rule in the applicable year's guidelines. Do not rely on a general statement such as “under 25” or “under 35” copied from a different category or previous cycle. Likewise, subject choices and degree equivalence must be checked against the official instructions.

5. Embassy recommendation and university recommendation

Embassy recommendation

Under the embassy route, the applicant applies through the Embassy of Japan in India during the announced recruitment period. The embassy conducts the first screening, which may include document review, written examinations and an interview depending on the category. Successful candidates proceed through the MEXT and university placement process described in the guidelines.

University recommendation

Under the university route, a Japanese university recommends candidates to MEXT under the relevant framework. Applicants should first check whether the institution is recruiting for the category and whether it accepts applications from the applicant's country and field. A professor's informal interest or email exchange is not itself a scholarship award.

The two routes have different calendars and procedures. An applicant should not assume that applying to a university and applying through the embassy are interchangeable or that both can be pursued simultaneously in every category.

6. Selection, exams and documents

Depending on the category, selection may include academic records, written tests, an interview, language assessment, research proposal and review of the applicant's motivation and future plans. The exact subjects and exam format are specified in the annual guidelines. Some categories may assess Japanese and English; others include subject-specific tests.

Common documents include application forms, academic transcripts, graduation or expected-graduation certificates, recommendation letters, medical certificate where required, research plan for relevant categories, language certificates and other category-specific records. The embassy may specify how many copies, what format and which documents must be submitted by post or in person.

For research applicants, the research plan should be focused and feasible. Explain the research question, background, method, relevance to Japan and the academic environment sought. Identify potential universities or supervisors based on genuine academic fit, while following the embassy's rules about placement preferences.

7. How Indian applicants should prepare

  1. Check the Embassy of Japan in India's MEXT page for the latest category and intake.
  2. Download the correct guidelines and forms; note the exact date-of-birth and education requirements.
  3. Confirm that your intended field is eligible and that you understand any preparatory or entrance examination stage.
  4. Prepare transcripts, certificates and translations in the format requested.
  5. Practise the relevant written examinations using official or clearly identified past papers where available.
  6. Build a clear study or research plan and prepare to explain your academic goals in an interview.
  7. Submit exactly as instructed and monitor official announcements for screening results.

8. Common mistakes

  • Applying under the wrong MEXT category.
  • Using a previous year's application dates or eligibility criteria.
  • Assuming a research student scholarship is identical to direct master's or doctoral admission.
  • Choosing a research theme without checking faculty or institutional fit.
  • Ignoring written examination requirements or subject restrictions.
  • Assuming the scholarship covers every initial or family expense.
  • Paying an intermediary who promises selection or a guaranteed professor acceptance.

2027 recruitment: embassy timelines and university recommendation are different

The official Study in Japan website explains that embassy-recommended recruitment for the 2027 intake generally took place in the previous year's April–May window, with first screening between May and July (the precise schedule varies by country and category). The FY2027 forms and guidelines are available on the official site. For Indian applicants, check the Embassy of Japan in India's own notice and results; do not send an application directly to MEXT in Japan.

University Recommendation is a separate route: a Japanese university nominates candidates under its allocated framework and internal schedule. It is not a second application that every student can submit independently to MEXT. Ask the university whether it has a recommendation quota for the relevant category, whether it accepts international applicants and when its internal selection takes place.

Official guidance: MEXT 2027 Research Student — Embassy Recommendation, MEXT 2027 Undergraduate — Embassy Recommendation and Embassy of Japan in India — scholarships.

For research applicants: make the proposal feasible, not just ambitious

The research plan is central to the research-student route. It should identify a specific question, explain what is already known, state the method and data/materials needed, and show why a Japanese host department is suitable. A broad topic such as “AI in healthcare” is not enough. Narrow it to a researchable problem that can be investigated within the available period and for which the host university has relevant expertise.

  1. Explain the academic foundation from your previous degree and any relevant project or publication.
  2. Identify a focused research question and two or three achievable objectives.
  3. Describe the method, data, laboratory access or fieldwork needed.
  4. Explain why the work should be done at the proposed Japanese institution.
  5. Check the faculty member's research area and whether they can supervise the proposed topic.
  6. Align your proposed study with the official MEXT form and word/space limits.

Prepare for screening: written tests, interview and supervisor fit

Depending on category, first screening can include document review, written examinations and an interview. The subjects and language tests differ; do not assume every master's applicant takes the same exam. For research applicants, be ready to explain your research plan, prior preparation, intended field and how you would progress from research student status to a degree programme if applicable.

A recommendation is not the final award or automatic degree admission

Embassy recommendation candidates pass through first screening and are then recommended to MEXT; final placement and award decisions follow the official process. Research-student candidates may need to contact universities for provisional acceptance during the prescribed stage. The host institution may require its own entrance examination before regular master's or doctoral enrolment. Read the annual guidelines carefully before assuming that the scholarship itself guarantees immediate degree admission.

If the 2027 embassy window has passed, prepare for the next cycle

  • Build the academic and research documents several months before the next embassy announcement.
  • Obtain official transcripts, degree certificates, grading scale and recommendation letters.
  • For research applicants, refine the proposal and identify suitable Japanese departments without assuming a professor's informal reply is a formal acceptance.
  • Monitor the Embassy of Japan in India and Study in Japan official pages for the next category-specific call.
  • Do not rely on third-party pages that keep last year's application dates online.

Frequently asked questions

Can Indian students apply for MEXT?

Yes, Indian applicants can apply to categories advertised for India, subject to the applicable annual guidelines and eligibility conditions.

Does MEXT cover tuition and living costs?

MEXT generally provides a monthly allowance and tuition/fee exemption, with travel support under its rules. Check the category-specific guideline for exact amounts and coverage.

Can I apply for a master's directly?

Many graduate applicants use the Research Students category, but the route and university admission process depend on the current rules. Research-student status does not automatically guarantee progression to a degree.

Is Japanese language compulsory?

Language requirements and preparatory study depend on category and programme. Some courses may be available in English, but applicants should not assume that all MEXT routes are English-only.

Are 2027 applications still open?

The Embassy of Japan in India's published 2027 recruitment page showed the listed 2026 application windows as closed. Check the embassy page for any later announcements and the next recruitment cycle.
