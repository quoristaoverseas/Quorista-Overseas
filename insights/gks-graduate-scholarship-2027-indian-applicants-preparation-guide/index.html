GKS Graduate Scholarship 2027: Preparation Guide for Indian Master's and PhD Applicants

The 2027 graduate call has not yet been published. Use this guide to prepare your academic profile, shortlist departments and organise documents—without mistaking last year's requirements for the next cycle's rules.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 15 min readPublished: 26 November 2026Reviewed: 26 November 2026

For the broader GKS programme structure and undergraduate/graduate distinctions, read our complete Global Korea Scholarship guide for Indian students. This article focuses specifically on preparation for the next graduate recruitment cycle.

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) Graduate programme is a competitive South Korean government scholarship for eligible international students pursuing Master's, doctoral and specified specialised graduate programmes. For Indian applicants, the challenge is not only meeting the academic threshold. Applicants must choose the correct track, match an eligible university and department, follow the annual document rules and present a credible academic plan.

Cycle status as of 26 November 2026: the official Study in Korea scholarship notice board currently shows the 2026 GKS Graduate guideline, published on 2 February 2026. The 2027 undergraduate call is a separate recruitment cycle. The 2027 GKS Graduate guideline, quota, Indian Embassy instructions and deadline should not be assumed until officially announced.

1. 2027 status: what is confirmed and what is not

The latest graduate guideline located on the official NIIED / Study in Korea notice board is for 2026. It confirms the programme's two broad submission routes: Embassy Track and University Track. It does not establish the 2027 application dates, university list, quota, application portal settings or document authentication rules.

NIIED's general GKS programme page describes the graduate framework, including the broad age and qualification conditions and the usual scholarship duration. These general descriptions are useful for early self-assessment, but the annual application guideline is the controlling document. A revised annual call can change conditions or introduce category-specific exceptions.

For India-specific instructions, monitor the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in India and the Korean Education Centre India. Also check the Study in Korea GKS notice board for the main annual guideline and university information files. Do not rely on an agent's expected date or a social-media calendar as an official opening announcement.

2. What GKS Graduate funds

The programme's published graduate structure commonly includes a Master's route of one year of Korean-language training followed by a two-year degree, and a doctoral route of one year of language training followed by a three-year degree. Specialised categories can have different arrangements; for example, the Global Network category is described separately and may not include the same language-training period. The exact duration and exemptions must be checked in the annual call.

GKS benefits can include round-trip airfare, Korean-language training fees, tuition and a monthly allowance. The annual guideline sets the actual rates, payment conditions and exceptions. Do not copy stipend figures from a previous year into a 2027 budget without checking the new guideline.

Even when tuition and a monthly allowance are covered, plan for expenses that may arise before the first payment or outside the award terms: passport and document preparation, travel to an interview or embassy, personal items, initial settlement, deposits or expenses for dependants. Do not assume dependants are funded.

3. Eligibility: profile checks before applying

NIIED's published general graduate information states that applicants must hold citizenship of a designated participating country, be under 40 years old as of 1 September of the inviting year (with a defined exception for certain eligible professors under 45), and hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree by the specified date. For the 2026 cycle, the guideline's key degree cut-off was 31 July 2026. The 2027 guide may set its own reference dates and detailed exceptions.

Academic performance

GKS uses an academic performance threshold and accepted grading evidence. Applicants should check the annual guideline for the precise minimum percentage, class-rank route, CGPA conversion formula and accepted official confirmation. Do not rely on an unofficial online CGPA converter or assume that an Indian university's percentage automatically maps to the required GKS scale.

Nationality and prior study restrictions

Nationality conditions generally apply to both the applicant and their parents; Korean citizenship or dual nationality can create disqualification. Prior GKS participation, previous study in Korea and other restrictions may also apply. Read the exclusions and not just the headline eligibility summary.

Expected graduates

Some annual calls allow applicants who are expected to graduate by a stated date. The exact deadline for final degree proof matters. If you are in the final semester, obtain an official expected-graduation certificate and current transcript, but do not assume the university can issue the final certificate by the deadline set in the next call.

Neither route is inherently easier. A strong university fit does not remove the competition at the embassy stage; equally, applying through a university does not guarantee nomination. The permitted combination of tracks and programmes is defined by the annual guideline. Never submit multiple applications unless the 2027 rules expressly allow the combination.

5. How to shortlist Korean universities

Start with the subject and degree, not the university's name recognition. For each department, verify:

  • It appears in the 2027 university information file for the correct track and category.
  • It accepts international GKS graduate applicants for your intended degree level.
  • Your Bachelor's or Master's background meets the department's prerequisites.
  • The research area, laboratory, supervisor or coursework genuinely matches your plan.
  • The language of instruction and any Korean-language expectations are realistic for you.
  • The university's own documents, portfolio, interview or additional forms can be completed by its deadline.

Use university websites and department pages to understand academic fit. Do not treat a university as GKS-eligible merely because it has admitted international students in the past; the annual NIIED university list controls participation for that cycle.

6. Documents and authentication planning

The annual guideline controls the required forms and evidence. A typical graduate application may include:

  • Application form, personal statement and study plan.
  • Recommendation letter(s) in the specified form and submission method.
  • Bachelor's degree certificate and complete transcripts; doctoral applicants may also need Master's degree evidence.
  • Proof of applicant and parents' nationality and family relationship.
  • Passport or other identity evidence, as instructed.
  • Language scores, awards, publications, research outputs or professional evidence where relevant.
  • Research proposal, portfolio or additional department-specific materials where required.
  • Medical self-assessment and other forms included in the annual application package.

For each document, record whether the initial stage accepts a scan, whether a certified translation is needed and whether the original or authenticated copy must later be submitted. Apostille and consular confirmation rules are not identical for every form. The 2027 guideline and Indian Embassy instructions must be checked before spending money on authentication.

Ask your university to issue a transcript that explains the grading scale or provides an official class rank if you intend to rely on that route. If your name is written differently across passport, degree and transcripts, obtain acceptable supporting evidence before the application window rather than trying to explain the mismatch at the last moment.

7. Writing a convincing study or research plan

A good study plan is specific to the proposed programme. It should show what you have studied, what academic question or skill you want to develop, why the Korean department is a sensible place to do it and what you intend to do after graduation.

For a Master's applicant

  • Connect your undergraduate subjects, projects or work to the intended Master's field.
  • Name relevant programme features, not just the city or the university's ranking.
  • Explain what you want to learn and how you will use it in a realistic career path.
  • If Korean-language study is part of the route, explain how you will approach it.

For a PhD applicant

  • State a research question that is narrow enough to investigate.
  • Explain the background literature, research gap, proposed method and likely limitations.
  • Identify why the department's expertise or facilities fit the project.
  • Show that the project is achievable within the scholarship period and that you understand any ethics or data-access issues.

Avoid a proposal that reads like a generic essay about Korea. Do not claim to have secured a supervisor's support unless that is documented and the university permits or expects prior contact.

9. Mistakes that can make an application ineligible

  • Using the undergraduate 2027 notice as if it were the graduate guideline.
  • Assuming 2026 deadlines, quotas, GPA conversions or university lists will repeat in 2027.
  • Choosing a department that is absent from the correct track's annual university file.
  • Ignoring parental nationality or prior-GKS restrictions.
  • Submitting a self-calculated grade conversion where official confirmation is required.
  • Missing a university's separate deadline or original-document delivery date.
  • Using generic or copied study plans that do not match the department.
  • Paying an intermediary for a guaranteed nomination or scholarship.

FAQs

Is GKS Graduate 2027 open for Indian applicants?

As of 26 November 2026, the official notice board located the 2026 GKS Graduate guideline but not the 2027 graduate call. Monitor NIIED and Indian Embassy notices for the new cycle.

Can I apply for a Master's if I am still completing my Bachelor's?

Expected graduates may be eligible under some annual rules if they can provide the required certificate and complete the degree by the specified cut-off. Wait for the 2027 date and evidence rules before concluding that you qualify.

Is IELTS compulsory for GKS?

Do not assume one universal answer. Language scores can strengthen an application or be required by a department, but the exact GKS category and university rules determine what is compulsory.

Is Embassy Track easier than University Track?

Neither is automatically easier. They have different first-round selectors, programme lists and application mechanics. Choose based on eligibility and academic fit.

Does GKS cover all living expenses?

It provides a defined support package, but you should check the annual allowance and plan for personal, initial and excluded costs. Do not assume dependants are funded.
