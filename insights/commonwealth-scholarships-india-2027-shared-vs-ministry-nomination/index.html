Commonwealth Scholarships for Indian Students 2027: Shared and Ministry-Nominated Routes

Understand the two distinct Commonwealth master's scholarship routes for Indian students in 2027: the CSC Shared Scholarship and India's Ministry-nominated Commonwealth Master's Scholarship, with separate deadlines, eligibility and application steps.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 14 min readUpdated: 25 November 2026

Indian applicants encounter two different Commonwealth master's scholarship routes for the 2027 UK academic year. They are both associated with the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC), but they are not the same award and do not use the same application timetable. One is the Ministry of Education's India-nominated 2027 Commonwealth Master's Scholarship; the other is the CSC's 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarship, for which candidates apply through CSC Central and participating universities.

Critical deadline distinction — checked 25 November 2026: For the Ministry-nominated 2027 Commonwealth Master's Scholarship, the CSC Central window closed on 20 October 2026, while SAKSHAT remains open until 30 November 2026. SAKSHAT alone cannot replace the missed CSC submission. Separately, the 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarship CSC application is open until 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT.

2. India's Ministry-nominated 2027 Commonwealth Master's Scholarship

The Ministry of Education notice invites Indian citizens who are permanent residents of India for full-time master's study commencing in September 2027. It states that applicants must submit through both CSC Central and the Ministry's SAKSHAT portal. The CSC Central window ran from 8 September to 20 October 2026. SAKSHAT is active from 30 October to 30 November 2026.

The Ministry notice says it will make 39 nominations to the CSC. A nomination is not a scholarship award: the final selection decision belongs to the CSC, and the Ministry says it has no role in the CSC's final selection.

Eligibility points in the Ministry notice

  • Indian citizen and permanent resident of India.
  • Available to begin study in the UK by September 2027.
  • By September 2027, hold a first degree at 2:1 level (the notice equates this to at least 60% in the Indian context), or a 2:2 degree (50–59%) plus a relevant master's degree.
  • One-year taught master's only; MBA programmes are not funded.
  • Must be unable to afford UK study without the scholarship and sign the required declaration.
  • No age limit is specified.
  • Applicants who spent more than six months abroad for study/training/specialisation are subject to the notice's return-to-India residence condition.

Important nomination criteria

The notice says applicants with a confirmed offer of admission by 16:00 on 30 November 2026 from a UK university listed on the CSC website will be considered for nomination. It then assigns points for an undertaking to return to India and work for at least two years, specified disadvantaged-background categories, gender diversity and the university's overall QS ranking. Nominations are made proportionately across the six development themes.

Applicants who already submitted to CSC Central by 20 October should read the Ministry notice and complete the SAKSHAT stage before its deadline, uploading the submitted CSC application PDF as instructed. If the CSC application was not submitted by 20 October, the Ministry notice says CSC cannot accept late applications; a SAKSHAT submission by itself does not repair that omission.

3. CSC Commonwealth Shared Scholarship 2027/28: a separate opportunity

The CSC's 2027/28 Shared Scholarship page says applications opened on 10 November 2026 and close at 16:00 GMT on 8 December 2026. India is on the CSC's eligible-country list. This is a separate application route from the Ministry's 2027 nomination exercise described above.

For Shared Scholarships, UK universities participate with approved courses and make the initial selection of candidates; the CSC subsequently checks eligibility and approves awards. Applicants must apply for an approved course at a participating university and also secure admission to that course. The CSC page says candidates may apply for more than one course and university but can accept only one Shared Scholarship award.

Who is eligible?

The CSC lists requirements including citizenship and permanent residence in an eligible Commonwealth country, availability to start in September/October 2027, a first degree at least equivalent to a 2:1 (or a lower second-class degree plus a relevant postgraduate qualification), inability to afford UK study without the scholarship, and not having studied or worked for more than one academic year in a high-income country. Read the full current criteria and any exceptions on the CSC page.

What does the Shared Scholarship cover?

The CSC currently lists approved return airfare, full approved tuition fees, a monthly stipend of £1,712 (or £2,000 for London metropolitan universities at the rates stated on the page), and certain study-related grants. Other allowances are conditional. These rates and entitlements are subject to the current handbook and award conditions; family support should not be treated as covering every dependant expense.

4. Which route can you still pursue?

  • You submitted the Ministry-route CSC application by 20 October: check the SAKSHAT instructions and submit the Indian-stage application by 30 November, ensuring you have the required confirmed admission offer by the stated cutoff.
  • You did not submit the Ministry-route CSC application by 20 October: do not assume SAKSHAT alone can qualify you for the Ministry nomination. Check the Ministry's current notice and contact its External Scholarship Division if you need clarification.
  • You want to apply for the 2027/28 Shared Scholarship: check the current approved-course list and participating universities, then apply through CSC Central by 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT and follow each university's separate admission process.

5. Prepare the right documents

The Ministry notice specifically calls for full transcripts of all higher-education qualifications, including up-to-date transcripts for qualifications still in progress, certified translations where needed, and references from at least two individuals. It also requires applicants to save a PDF of the submitted CSC application for upload to SAKSHAT.

The Shared Scholarship application asks for a detailed study plan, personal statement, employment history, referees and explanations of study objectives and future career plans. Exact supporting documents and course requirements vary. Prepare accurate records and do not embellish employment, publications, leadership or impact.

6. Common mistakes

  • Assuming the Ministry-nominated award and Shared Scholarship are the same scheme.
  • Believing that SAKSHAT submission can substitute for the CSC application that closed on 20 October.
  • Using the Ministry nomination criteria as if they automatically applied to the Shared Scholarship.
  • Assuming a scholarship application is also a university admission application.
  • Choosing a course not on the relevant approved list or missing the university's separate admission deadline.
  • Calling a Ministry nomination a confirmed scholarship award.

Frequently asked questions

Are the Ministry-nominated Commonwealth Master's Scholarship and Shared Scholarship the same?

No. They are separate routes with different application processes, timelines and selection arrangements.

Can I still apply through SAKSHAT if I missed the Ministry-route CSC deadline?

The Ministry notice says the CSC Central application closed on 20 October and that late CSC applications cannot be accepted. SAKSHAT alone should not be treated as a replacement for the CSC submission.

Is the 2027/28 Shared Scholarship still open?

Yes. The CSC page states a deadline of 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT. Check the current approved courses and university admission requirements immediately.

Does the Ministry nominate every eligible applicant?

No. The notice provides for 39 nominations and sets out a points-based nomination process. CSC makes the final scholarship selection.

Does the Shared Scholarship require SAKSHAT?

The CSC Shared Scholarship application page describes an application through CSC Central and does not state the Ministry SAKSHAT step. Do not transfer requirements from the separate Ministry-nominated scheme.
