South Korea Spring 2027 Admissions: Universities Still Listed for International Students

A checked list of Spring 2027 South Korean university admissions for international students, with degree levels, official portal deadlines, English-track and language-course distinctions.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 12 min readPublished: 25 November 2026Reviewed: 25 November 2026

Spring 2027 applications in South Korea are already open at several institutions. The useful question for an applicant in India is not simply which universities are accepting applications, but which degree level, programme language and document route are actually open—and whether there is enough time to complete the application properly.

Checked on 25 November 2026: The Korean government's Study in Korea application portal lists active Spring 2027 opportunities at Inje University, Mokwon University, Korea Nazarene University, Cha University and Dong-Eui University. Their deadlines and study levels differ. Some require physical original documents in addition to online submission.

They are not a guarantee that every department has vacancies or that a university will accept a late or incomplete file. Use the institution's current admission guide as the controlling document.

2. Inje University: a current graduate route, but an urgent deadline

Inje University's Spring 2027 international graduate guide covers master's, doctoral and combined master's–doctoral admission. The application window is 2–27 November 2026. Applicants first complete the online application through Study in Korea and then send the required original documents by post or submit them in person. The original package must arrive at the university by 27 November; a courier dispatch receipt dated 27 November is not enough.

The guide lists programmes in medicine, global MBA, sciences, engineering, computer engineering and other areas. It marks some programmes as having 50% or more English use and identifies Global MBA, Industrial and Management, and Computer Engineering as 100% English-track in the published table. “English track” is not a universal guarantee that every course or supervisor interaction will be entirely in English; contact the department before applying.

Applicants need to check the detailed guide for eligibility, language evidence, recommendation letters, degree documents, financial proof and the physical submission address. The separate South Korea without TOPIK guide explains why a programme's teaching language and the language evidence required for admission are not interchangeable.

3. Mokwon University: English-track undergraduate intake

The Study in Korea portal lists Mokwon University's Spring 2027 undergraduate freshman admission for international students under an English track, with an application period of 23 November–23 December 2026. It also lists a separate Korean-track transfer route for the same period.

This is potentially relevant to school-leavers or applicants seeking a first bachelor's degree, not a master's applicant. Before treating it as a realistic option, obtain the current Mokwon admission guide and verify the available majors, school-leaving qualification, academic records, English proficiency evidence, tuition, scholarship conditions, interview and document delivery deadline. The portal listing alone does not establish the detailed admission requirements.

4. Korea Nazarene University: undergraduate route through 8 January

The national portal lists Korea Nazarene University's Spring 2027 international undergraduate freshman admission from 2 November 2026 to 8 January 2027. A Korean-language training course is listed separately with the same dates. These are distinct admissions: one is a bachelor's degree route and the other is language training.

Applicants should not assume the language-course listing is a direct pathway into a degree or that completion guarantees progression. Ask for the current guide, course duration, attendance rules, tuition, refund policy, language level and whether progression requires a fresh degree application.

5. Cha University and Dong-Eui University: short-deadline listings

Cha University appears on the portal with Spring 2027 undergraduate freshman admission and a separate master's listing, both showing 3–27 November 2026. The listing does not by itself confirm that every master's department is available to international applicants or that the applicant's intended subject is included. Confirm the programme and delivery deadline directly with the university before spending on document certification or courier services.

Dong-Eui University appears with two Spring 2027 language-related listings: an EAP (D-4-7) programme and a Korean-language course (D-4), both showing 9–30 November 2026. These are not degree admissions. The visa classification displayed in a programme name should not be treated as a visa approval; the institution's admission documents and Korean immigration/embassy assessment determine the applicable process.

6. Which of these options is relevant to you?

  • Already hold a bachelor's degree and want a master's: investigate Inje first only if the subject, language and document deadlines are feasible; check Cha University's master's listing with the department.
  • Still seeking a bachelor's degree: Mokwon's English-track freshman listing and Korea Nazarene University's undergraduate listing are the relevant entries to investigate.
  • Want Korean-language study rather than a degree: examine the separate Dong-Eui and Korea Nazarene language-course listings, but assess cost, duration and progression separately.
  • Need an English-taught graduate programme: inspect the department-level English-track details. A university-wide claim or a portal label is not enough.

7. Prepare documents without losing time

Requirements vary by university and degree level, but applicants should be ready to obtain:

  • Passport identity page and recent photograph.
  • School certificates and marksheets for undergraduate applications; bachelor's degree certificate and full transcripts for master's applications.
  • Expected-graduation evidence if the degree is not yet awarded, where the university permits it.
  • Language test certificate or other language evidence specifically accepted by the programme.
  • Study plan, personal statement, recommendation letters, CV or portfolio where requested.
  • Birth certificate or family-relation documents where needed to prove applicant and parents' nationality.
  • Bank balance or financial sponsorship evidence in the exact format and validity period required by the institution.
  • Official translations and apostille/consular confirmation where required.

Inje's guide, for example, requires original documents, English or Korean documents (or notarised translations), and a proof-of-financial-affordability document issued within 30 days of application. Its listed bank certificate minimum is KRW 16,000,000 in the applicant's or parent's account, or an affidavit from the expected Inje academic advisor. Other universities may use different figures and formats.

8. Admission is not the same as a student visa

Even after an offer, applicants must complete the university's registration and visa-document process. Degree programmes and language courses may involve different student-status categories and different supporting documents. The visa decision is made by the competent Korean authorities, not by the Study in Korea portal or an education agent.

Before paying a deposit, verify the institution's current tuition and refund terms, the expected issuance of the standard admission documents, the applicable visa category, financial proof, accommodation plan and the Korean mission's current instructions for applicants in India. Do not book non-refundable travel based only on a portal listing or conditional offer.

9. What to do next: a realistic 48-hour checklist

  1. Choose the correct route: undergraduate degree, graduate degree or language course.
  2. Open the current university admission guide and confirm that your intended department is accepting international applicants.
  3. Check the actual document-receipt deadline—not just the online application closing date.
  4. Email the international office if the guide is unclear about English-track availability, language proof, Indian qualifications or courier arrival.
  5. Prepare originals, translations and financial documents exactly as requested; do not assume scans alone are sufficient.
  6. Apply through the official portal and retain the application number, payment proof and courier tracking.

Before applying or paying a fee, download the latest guide from the university's own admissions office and confirm that the exact department is recruiting. Do not shortlist by university name alone

  • If you need a master's: the undergraduate rounds at Gachon, BUFS, Jeonju, Kookmin and Yeungnam are not relevant. The reported December graduate routes include Sookmyung, Kwangwoon, Kyung Hee (restricted to specified science/engineering departments), Gachon and Dongseo.
  • If you need English-medium study: confirm the exact degree, department and course language. An “English track” label does not mean every class, elective, administrative process or thesis-supervision arrangement will be in English.
  • If you do not have TOPIK: check whether the programme accepts IELTS/TOEFL/CEFR, an interview, a Korean language course completion certificate or a later TOPIK submission. The accepted alternatives differ by university and sometimes by department.
  • If you are applying from India: check whether original documents must arrive in Korea, whether apostilles or consular confirmation are required, and whether the remaining time is realistic for courier and visa processing.

Some late rounds can leave very little time before March 2027 registration. Ask the university when it expects to issue the Certificate of Admission and whether there is sufficient time for the applicable visa process in India. An admission result is not a visa, and a published deadline does not guarantee that a particular applicant's documents will be processed in time.

Frequently asked questions

Which South Korean universities are still listed for Spring 2027 admission?

As checked on 25 November 2026, the Study in Korea portal lists opportunities at Inje, Mokwon, Korea Nazarene, Cha and Dong-Eui universities. The degree level and route differ by institution.

Can a master's applicant apply to Mokwon's English-track listing?

The listing described here is undergraduate freshman admission. It is not a master's programme.

Is Inje University's Spring 2027 graduate application still open?

The published window is 2–27 November 2026, but original documents must arrive by 27 November. Applicants should contact Inje immediately to confirm whether a package can reach the office in time.

Does an English-track listing mean TOPIK is never required?

No. Confirm the language criteria for the exact department and the graduation requirements. Teaching language, admission language evidence and any later Korean-language requirements are separate questions.

Does a D-4 language-course listing guarantee a later degree admission?

No. Language training and degree admission are separate processes. Progression, if available, depends on the university's rules and a new admission decision.

Does admission guarantee a Korean student visa?

No. The visa decision is separate and depends on the applicant's documents and current Korean immigration and embassy requirements.
