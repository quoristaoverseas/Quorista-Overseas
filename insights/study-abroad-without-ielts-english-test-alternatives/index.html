Study Abroad Without IELTS: English Test Alternatives

Explore IELTS alternatives for Indian students applying abroad, including TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo, Cambridge tests and university-specific waivers.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 10 min readPublished: 25 November 2026

IELTS is widely used for international admissions, but it is not the only English-language test accepted by universities. Depending on the institution, course and applicant profile, other tests or alternative evidence may be accepted.

Important: “Without IELTS” does not necessarily mean “without English-language proof.” Admission rules, visa rules and programme requirements are separate and can change.

Is IELTS mandatory for studying abroad?

There is no universal IELTS requirement covering every country or university. Universities may accept different tests, English-medium education evidence, interviews or internal assessments. Some programmes insist on IELTS or another specified test even when the institution offers waivers elsewhere.

Alternatives to IELTS

  • TOEFL iBT: widely used for academic English assessment; confirm current format and accepted score.
  • PTE Academic: accepted by many institutions, but not all departments or visa pathways.
  • Duolingo English Test: accepted by selected institutions and programmes; verify directly.
  • Cambridge English qualifications: some institutions accept specified certificates and levels.
  • LanguageCert or other approved tests: acceptance varies.
  • Medium of Instruction (MOI): a letter or academic document showing prior study was taught in English, where accepted.
  • University interview or internal test: some institutions assess proficiency directly.

Can an MOI certificate replace IELTS?

Sometimes. An MOI certificate is not a global waiver. Universities may accept it only for certain degrees, applicants, academic levels or countries of prior study. They may specify wording, duration or documentation required. Ask admissions to confirm in writing before relying on it.

Does this vary by country?

Yes. Requirements are country- and institution-specific. Universities in the UK, US and many European destinations may accept alternative tests or waivers in certain circumstances. Other countries may impose separate language-document rules for visa or recognition processes. Do not assume a university waiver automatically satisfies immigration requirements.

Admission requirements are not visa requirements

A university may waive IELTS for admission, but the visa process, scholarship rules or regulated programme may require additional evidence. Verify both institution language policy and visa documentation.

How to verify an IELTS waiver

  1. Open the programme-specific admissions page.
  2. Identify accepted tests, scores and waiver conditions.
  3. Check whether MOI is accepted and whether a specific format is required.
  4. Confirm visa, scholarship or professional accreditation requirements separately.
  5. Ask admissions for written confirmation if unclear.

FAQs

Can I study abroad without IELTS?

Yes, in some cases, if the university accepts another test or alternative evidence. This depends on programme and applicant.

Is an MOI certificate enough?

Some institutions accept it, but it is not universally accepted and may be subject to conditions.

Can I use Duolingo instead of IELTS?

Some institutions accept Duolingo English Test, while others do not. Check current course-specific requirements.

Does an IELTS waiver guarantee a visa?

No. Admission and visa decisions are separate and depend on applicable requirements.

Where to check

  • Official university and programme admissions pages
  • Official immigration or embassy/consulate websites
  • Official test-provider pages for current formats and scores

Editorial note: General guidance only. Requirements vary and change. Confirm with official sources before making decisions. No admission or visa outcome is guaranteed.
