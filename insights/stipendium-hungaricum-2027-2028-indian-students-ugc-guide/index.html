Stipendium Hungaricum 2027/28 for Indian Students: UGC Route, Eligibility and Funding Guide

The official programme announced on 26 November 2026 that the 2027/28 call will open soon. Here is what Indian applicants can verify now—and what must wait for the detailed call and UGC instructions.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 14 min readPublished: 26 November 2026Reviewed: 26 November 2026

For a comparison with other major European funding routes, see our European scholarships guide for Indian students. Stipendium Hungaricum is the Hungarian Government's principal higher-education scholarship programme for international students. It can support full-degree Bachelor's, Master's and doctoral study at participating Hungarian institutions. For Indian applicants, an important detail is often overlooked: eligibility is linked to the bilateral sending-partner arrangement, and the University Grants Commission (UGC) is listed as India's responsible authority.

Current status — 26 November 2026: the official Stipendium Hungaricum website published a notice today saying applications for the 2027/28 programme will open soon. The notice does not yet provide the detailed call, exact deadline or final India-specific instructions. Do not treat a previous cycle's deadline as confirmed for 2027/28.

1. 2027/28 status: call opening soon

The Tempus Public Foundation's announcement dated 26 November 2026 confirms that the 2027/28 application period is approaching and invites prospective applicants to explore programmes through the Study Finder. It does not yet announce the opening date, closing date, final programme annexes or country-specific nomination details.

That means applicants can use the time now to research degree programmes, check academic fit, collect records and prepare referees. The final eligibility and deadline decisions must wait for the 2027/28 Call for Applications and the Indian sending-partner notice.

Use the official Stipendium Hungaricum website as the primary source. Be cautious with third-party websites that already publish a firm 2027/28 deadline without linking to the official call.

2. India's UGC sending-partner route

The official Stipendium Hungaricum partner page lists India — University Grants Commission (UGC) as the sending partner. The page provides UGC contact information and describes the study types and fields currently available under the India agreement.

The sending-partner arrangement matters because Stipendium Hungaricum is based on bilateral education agreements. Applicants can apply only for the scholarship types and study fields determined by the agreement with their sending partner. A course appearing in the Hungarian Study Finder does not automatically mean it is eligible for an Indian applicant under the current bilateral agreement.

Monitor two separate official sources:

  • Tempus Public Foundation / Stipendium Hungaricum: the annual call, application system, university programme annexes, scholarship conditions and overall deadlines.
  • UGC: the Indian nomination process, any additional application or document requirements, and the Indian deadline if one is set.

Do not assume that UGC's role is merely a formality. The programme's application guidance explains that only nominated applicants can proceed in the selection process. The sending partner's instructions are therefore part of the application, not an optional extra.

Confirm the programme's degree level, language, entry requirements and Indian eligibility before investing time in an application.

4. How the application and nomination process works

  1. Review the annual call. Download the 2027/28 BA/BSc and MA/MSc or doctoral call, annexes, application guide and statement forms from the official site.
  2. Check the India partner conditions. Confirm that your degree level and chosen field are included for India and identify whether UGC requires a separate nomination application or documents.
  3. Create the Tempus Public Foundation online application. Select only eligible programmes and upload the documents in the required format.
  4. Complete the UGC / sending-partner process. Follow the Indian partner's separate deadline and submission method if required. A Tempus application alone should not be assumed to complete India's nomination stage.
  5. Submit and retain proof. Confirm the online application status shows submitted/resubmitted as required and save the final PDF or confirmation.
  6. Wait for nomination and university assessment. The sending partner nominates candidates; universities conduct their admission assessment or examination later in the process.
  7. Review the final award conditions. A nomination or university admission stage is not the same as final scholarship selection.

The programme's official FAQ says applicants do not need to have a university admission offer by the scholarship application deadline. The university admission process takes place later. It also states that applicants cannot simply apply directly to a university for a Stipendium scholarship: the online programme application and sending-partner nomination route must be followed.

Do not mix up the two deadlines. The Tempus application deadline and any UGC-specific deadline may not be identical. If the UGC notice sets an earlier date or a separate document submission, meeting only the Tempus deadline may not be enough.

5. Eligibility and academic preparation

The 2027/28 call will provide the controlling eligibility rules. Applicants should expect the standard checks to include citizenship, previous education, degree-level prerequisites, academic records, language requirements, age or other category-specific conditions where stated, and any restrictions in the India bilateral agreement.

Academic fit

For Bachelor's applicants, check the accepted secondary-school qualification, subject prerequisites and any entrance examination. For Master's applicants, check whether the previous Bachelor's degree is considered relevant and whether specific courses or credits are required. For doctoral applicants, confirm research-field fit, supervisor arrangements if required, proposal format and any interview.

Language requirements

Some programmes are taught in English and others in Hungarian or another language. The university may require IELTS, TOEFL, another recognised certificate, an interview or other proof. Do not assume that a medium-of-instruction letter is accepted by every university or that the scholarship itself waives the university's language requirement. Check each programme page and its admission annex.

Expected graduates

If you are in the final year of your current degree, check whether the 2027/28 call permits expected graduates and the latest date by which the final certificate must be submitted. Prepare a current transcript and an official expected-graduation letter. Do not assume an admission or scholarship decision can be finalised without the required completed qualification evidence.

6. What the scholarship covers—and what it does not

The programme's currently published standard benefits for Bachelor's, Master's and one-tier Master's students include tuition-free education, a monthly stipend of HUF 43,700, either a free dormitory place or a housing contribution of HUF 40,000 per month, and medical coverage under the stated Hungarian and supplementary insurance arrangements.

Doctoral students have a different monthly stipend structure: the currently published amount is HUF 140,000 per month in the first phase and HUF 180,000 per month in the second phase, alongside the accommodation and insurance provisions described by the programme. Verify the 2027/28 call for any changes before relying on these figures.

The programme itself warns that the stipend and housing contribution are only contributions to living expenses and that scholars may need additional personal resources. This is particularly important in Budapest and other higher-cost locations. Do a city-specific monthly budget before accepting an award.

7. Documents to prepare

  • Passport and proof of citizenship.
  • Secondary-school or university certificates and complete transcripts, according to degree level.
  • Grading scale or official explanation of marks/CGPA.
  • Current transcript and expected-graduation certificate if still studying.
  • CV or résumé, statement of purpose and motivation letter where requested.
  • Language certificate or other evidence accepted by the selected university.
  • Recommendation letters in the specified format.
  • Doctoral research proposal and supervisor-related documents if required.
  • Medical certificate using the current official form, where required.
  • Certified translations or authenticated copies only where the call or partner requires them.

Keep a separate checklist for Tempus and UGC. A document uploaded to one system is not automatically received by the other. Make sure names, dates, qualification titles and grades are consistent across documents.

8. Choosing a suitable Hungarian programme

Use the official Study Finder to discover programmes, but verify every shortlisted course against the India partner's permitted levels and fields. Then read the university's own programme page. A scholarship-eligible field does not guarantee that your specific previous degree meets the admission prerequisites.

For each option, record:

  • Exact degree title, level, language of instruction and campus.
  • Whether the programme appears in the 2027/28 annex for India.
  • Required previous subjects, credits, grades, portfolio or entrance examination.
  • Language evidence and any interview.
  • Programme length and whether it is full degree or partial study.
  • Housing availability and realistic city living costs.
  • Any additional costs not covered by the scholarship.

Do not select a course only because its title sounds related to your degree. For example, a Master's in computer science may require programming and mathematics credits that a general business degree does not provide. The university's formal entry criteria matter.

9. What to do before the call opens

  1. Today: bookmark the official 2027/28 call page and the India partner page.
  2. Next few days: build a shortlist of eligible degree levels and fields; do not assume every Study Finder result is available to India.
  3. Prepare records: request transcripts, grading-scale details, expected-graduation evidence and referee availability.
  4. Check language: identify the requirements of each chosen university and book a test only if it is genuinely needed.
  5. When the call appears: compare the main call, programme annexes, India partner page and UGC notice line by line.
  6. Before submitting: verify both Tempus and UGC deadlines, file formats, nomination rules and final submission status.

FAQs

Has Stipendium Hungaricum 2027/28 opened?

On 26 November 2026, the official programme announced that applications will open soon. The detailed call and exact deadline were not included in that announcement.

Is UGC India's sending partner?

Yes. The official Stipendium Hungaricum partner page lists the University Grants Commission as India's responsible authority. Check UGC's own 2027/28 notice for the Indian nomination procedure.

Can Indian students apply for every subject?

No. The currently published India profile lists broad priority fields and excludes one-tier Master's. The exact 2027/28 annex and UGC instructions must be checked.

Do I need a university offer before applying?

The programme FAQ says applicants do not need a university admission offer by the scholarship application deadline; the university admission assessment takes place later. Follow the new call for any changes.

Does the stipend cover all living costs?

No. The programme explicitly describes the stipend and accommodation contribution as contributions. You may need additional funds, especially in higher-cost cities.
