UK University Scholarships for Indian Students 2027: Manchester, Bristol and Exeter

Compare confirmed 2027 UK university scholarships for Indian students, including Manchester's £12,000 award, Bristol Think Big and Exeter's £10,000 India scholarship, with eligibility and deadlines.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 12 min readUpdated: 25 November 2026

For Indian students targeting September 2027 entry, several UK universities have already published scholarship terms. The important question is not just the headline award value: applicants need to know whether the award is open to India, whether a separate scholarship form is required, which courses are excluded, and whether the discount is renewable or only for the first year.

Scope: this guide focuses on university-published 2027/28 opportunities at Manchester, Bristol and Exeter. These are tuition-fee awards, not interchangeable with a fully funded scholarship. Always check the exact course and fee status.

2. University of Manchester: £12,000 master's award for Indian students

Manchester's Global Futures Scholarships 2027 page lists a £12,000 tuition-fee discount for master's applicants domiciled in India. Applicants must be international fee-paying students who have predominantly lived in India during the previous three years, and must hold a conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible full-time, on-campus master's course starting in September 2027.

The scholarship form becomes available after the applicant has applied and received an offer. The published deadline is 25 April 2027, and applicants must be self-funded rather than sponsored. Manchester says Indian awards will be allocated in two rounds: half by 15 March and the remainder by 1 June 2027. An applicant unsuccessful in the first round is automatically reconsidered in the second; a new application is not required.

Important exclusions include MBA, MPhil, several architecture programmes, PGCE, clinical medicine and dentistry, and some master's-by-research courses. Check the full terms and conditions for the exact programme before applying.

3. University of Bristol: Think Big for September 2027

Bristol confirms that Think Big scholarship applications for September 2027 entry are open. The application deadline is 19 March 2027 at 1 pm UK time. The scheme supports eligible international undergraduate and postgraduate students with tuition-fee funding; award amounts differ by scholarship and level, so do not assume one headline value applies to every Indian applicant.

You must already have applied for an eligible September 2027 course, but Bristol says you do not need to have received an admission offer before submitting the scholarship application. The international scholarship form also considers applicants for other listed awards for which they are eligible, including GREAT and named bursaries. Eligible programmes are in-person undergraduate courses and one-year postgraduate taught or master's-by-research courses. Online, distance and part-time programmes are excluded.

For a master's applicant, the practical sequence is: apply to the course, complete the international scholarship form by the deadline, then monitor both admission and scholarship decisions. Confirm the award amount and conditions in the specific scholarship terms.

4. University of Exeter: £10,000 India postgraduate taught award

Exeter lists a £10,000 tuition-fee scholarship for Indian passport holders enrolling in eligible postgraduate taught programmes in September 2027 or January 2028. The award is for the first year only and is automatically allocated to eligible applicants; a separate scholarship application is not required. The published deadline is 31 August 2027.

Eligibility requires an offer for an eligible master's programme, international fee status and self-funded status. The award is a tuition-fee reduction, not a cash payment for living expenses, and it cannot be combined with another University of Exeter scholarship. A deposit and meeting the conditions of the offer are part of the award conditions.

Exeter's India page also lists separate awards, including a £15,000 Exeter Excellence Scholarship for some September 2027 postgraduate taught applicants, but these are competitive and have their own criteria. Do not add the £15,000 and £10,000 awards together or assume both apply to one student; check combination rules and eligible course lists.

5. Which option is more realistic?

  • Have a Manchester offer and strong academic results: check course exclusions and apply for the £12,000 award before 25 April 2027.
  • Applying to Bristol: submit the Think Big form by 19 March even if the admission decision has not arrived, provided you have applied to an eligible course.
  • Prefer automatic consideration: Exeter's £10,000 India award does not require a separate scholarship form, but you still need an eligible offer and must meet the self-funded conditions.

Compare the net tuition after the award, not just the scholarship headline. Add deposits, accommodation, living costs, visa fees, health surcharge, travel and any remaining tuition. A £10,000 tuition reduction can still leave a substantial funding requirement.

6. Application plan and documents

  1. Shortlist the exact course and confirm it appears on the university's eligible-course list.
  2. Check whether the scholarship is automatic or requires a separate form.
  3. Apply for admission early enough to meet any offer-holder deadline.
  4. Prepare transcripts, degree certificates or expected-result evidence, CV, personal statement and references as required by the course.
  5. Write a focused scholarship statement that demonstrates academic merit, course fit and the contribution you can make.
  6. Check if the award is renewable, conditional on grades, restricted to self-funded students or incompatible with other funding.

Frequently asked questions

Are these scholarships fully funded?

No. The awards described here are tuition-fee discounts or contributions. They do not automatically cover accommodation, living costs, travel or all other expenses.

Can Indian students apply to all three?

Indian students are explicitly included in the Manchester and Exeter India criteria. Bristol's Think Big is open to eligible overseas-fee students; confirm the exact award and course terms.

Do I need an admission offer before applying for Bristol Think Big?

You must have applied for an eligible course, but Bristol says you do not need to have received an offer before submitting the scholarship form.

Does Exeter require a separate scholarship application?

No separate application is required for the published £10,000 India postgraduate taught award; eligible applicants are considered automatically, subject to the terms.

Can I combine university awards?

Do not assume so. Exeter states that its India award cannot be combined with another Exeter scholarship. Check the relevant university terms for every other award.
