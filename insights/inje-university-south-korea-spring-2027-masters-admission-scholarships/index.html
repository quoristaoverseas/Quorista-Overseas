Inje University South Korea: Spring 2027 Master's Admission, English Tracks and Scholarships

A detailed look at Inje University's Spring 2027 international graduate admission, English-track departments, IELTS and TOPIK criteria, tuition, scholarships, financial proof and urgent document deadline.

✦ Quorista Overseas Editorial Team◷ 14 min readPublished: 25 November 2026Reviewed: 25 November 2026

Inje University has published its Spring 2027 international graduate admission guide for master's, PhD and combined master's–doctoral applicants. The opportunity is unusually time-sensitive: the online application and the arrival of original supporting documents are both due by 27 November 2026. As of 25 November, applicants outside Korea should not assume there is enough time for an international courier unless the university confirms it.

Key facts: 2–27 November 2026 application period; original documents must arrive by 27 November; interviews are scheduled between 30 November and 24 December; results are scheduled for 8 January 2027. The university may change the schedule. This is a specific Spring 2027 opportunity, not a general year-round intake.

1. Which graduate programmes are listed?

The official guide includes master's, doctoral and combined master's–doctoral options across medicine, humanities and social sciences, natural sciences, engineering and arts, plus interdisciplinary and cooperative programmes. The published department list includes:

  • Business: Global MBA (100% English track), with listed areas including human resources, marketing, operations management, finance, accounting and international business strategy.
  • Computing and engineering: Computer Engineering (100% English track), Industrial and Management (100% English track), and several engineering departments marked as 50% or more English use, including mechanical and electronic engineering, civil/environmental/fire prevention engineering, and nanoscience and engineering.
  • Science and health: Biological Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, Pharmacy, Public Health, Integrative Biomedical Sciences and Engineering, and other specialist programmes. Several are marked 50% or more English use.
  • Other options: Medicine, Medical Sciences, physical therapy, social welfare, public administration, design, music and selected interdisciplinary fields. Their language and programme conditions vary considerably.

The guide's “English Track” column is a departmental indicator, not a promise that every class, supervisor meeting, clinical placement or administrative process will be in English. A department marked “X” should not be treated as English-taught simply because the university accepts international students. Contact the department and ask for the current course language and supervision availability before applying.

2. Who can apply?

The guide's general nationality route requires the applicant and both parents to hold foreign citizenship. It also describes an alternative education route based on completing the equivalent of 16 years of primary, secondary and college education. The applicant must meet the university's nationality/education eligibility conditions and the relevant degree-level requirements.

  • Master's: must already hold, or be expected to receive, a bachelor's degree by the deadline set by Inje University.
  • PhD: must already hold, or be expected to receive, both a bachelor's and master's degree by the university's deadline.
  • Combined master's–PhD: follows the master's-level entry eligibility described in the guide.

For Indian applicants, the university's expected-degree wording does not remove the need to submit official degree evidence by the relevant deadline. The guide has special instructions for applicants whose graduation is pending, including a requirement for foreign-degree applicants to submit an apostilled degree certificate or Korean consular confirmation before the admission-result notification. If you are in your final semester, obtain written clarification from Inje immediately.

3. English or Korean language requirements

Applicants must demonstrate Korean or English proficiency under one of the listed routes.

LanguagePublished minimum examples
KoreanTOPIK Level 3 or above; KIIP Level 3 or above; King Sejong Institute Intermediate Korean Level 1 or above (offline class); or Inje Korean Language and Culture Center Korean Level 4 or above.
EnglishTOEFL iBT 71 or above; IELTS 5.5 or above; CEFR B2 or above; or NEW TEPS 326 or above.

The guide says the listed English proficiency requirements may be relaxed for GKS students, foreign government scholarship students and special cases approved after deliberation by the Graduate School Admission Committee. That is not a general MOI waiver for all Indian applicants. If you plan to use an English-medium degree or another certificate, get confirmation from the department before applying.

4. Application method and deadline: read this carefully

  1. Create an account on the official Study in Korea portal and complete the Inje University application for the correct department and degree level.
  2. Enter the required personal, family, academic, financial, language, study-plan and work-experience information.
  3. Upload the scanned supporting documents and submit the online application.
  4. After receiving the application number, send the required original documents separately by post or deliver them in person to Inje University's Office of International Affairs.
  5. Ensure the complete original package arrives by 27 November 2026. The guide explicitly says documents must arrive by the deadline.

Interviews for applicants who pass the first screening are scheduled from 30 November to 24 December 2026. The department will notify applicants individually of the date and whether the interview is in person or online. The guide schedules admission-result notification for 8 January 2027. Tuition payment and visa processing dates are to be announced to successful applicants.

5. Documents and financial proof

The guide's checklist includes the following for master's applicants:

  • Application form and passport photograph.
  • One recommendation letter from a professor, academic adviser or department chair at the previously attended university or graduate school.
  • Bachelor's degree certificate (or expected degree evidence where applicable) and original undergraduate transcripts, including transcripts for any prior two- or three-year college study.
  • Official English or Korean language certificate, if applicable; language test scores must be within two years of the application deadline.
  • Applicant's passport copy, if applicable, and documents proving the nationality of the applicant and parents, such as birth or family-relation certificates.
  • Education-verification consent form and the relevant application fee.
  • Full-time scholarship application form, where applying for the relevant scholarship.
  • Proof of financial affordability.

For the financial-affordability document, the guide specifies an original bank deposit certificate showing at least KRW 16,000,000 in the applicant's or parent's account, or a financial-support affidavit from the expected Inje academic adviser using the university's form. The document must have been issued within 30 days of the application date. This is Inje's published admission-document requirement; do not assume it replaces any separate visa-stage financial evidence.

Documents must be originals, in English or Korean, or accompanied by notarised English/Korean translations. The guide also gives apostille or Korean consular-confirmation requirements for foreign degree certificates. Applicants should not assume that ordinary photocopies or self-downloaded portal screenshots are acceptable.

6. Tuition fees and scholarship possibilities

The guide's tuition table is based on the university's 2026 Spring semester figures and says amounts are subject to change. It lists the following standard master's tuition by broad academic field:

A separate admission fee of KRW 802,000 is listed. Global Nursing is shown separately at KRW 8,500,000 per semester and marked ineligible for the scholarship in the guide. Confirm the current 2027 bill with the university before calculating affordability.

50% tuition waiver

The guide describes a 50% tuition waiver for full-time students recommended by the academic adviser or department chair. It lists no additional academic publication requirement for this 50% waiver. However, it is not automatic for every admitted student: full-time status and the relevant recommendation remain important.

85% tuition waiver

The 85% waiver is more demanding. The student must be full-time, be recommended by the adviser or department chair, maintain a GPA of at least 3.5/4.5 after admission and fulfil publication or academic-presentation obligations specified by the field. The guide states that graduation is not possible if the obligations are not met, and the scholarship must be returned if the recipient gives up full-time status. It is therefore misleading to advertise this as an unconditional 85% scholarship.

BK21 research support

The guide identifies selected departments participating in the Brain Korea 21 programme. It states that eligible graduate students in participating departments may receive monthly stipends of KRW 1,000,000 or more for master's students and KRW 1,600,000 or more for PhD students, plus research-achievement incentives. This is department- and participation-dependent support, not a guaranteed stipend for every Inje graduate student. Confirm whether your exact department, supervisor and research position qualify.

7. Visa process: what the offer does and does not mean

The guide says accepted applicants should proceed with the student-visa process through the Korean Embassy or immigration office after tuition payment and the university's instructions. It does not promise an e-visa, a Confirmation of Visa Issuance (CCVI) route or a visa outcome for every applicant. Applicants in India should confirm the current procedure and required documents with Inje and the competent Korean mission before assuming the precise route.

Admission, scholarship selection, university financial-document checks and visa approval are separate decisions. A tuition waiver does not automatically eliminate the need to demonstrate funds for living costs or other visa requirements.

8. Is Inje a realistic option for you?

  • Potentially worth investigating: applicants with a relevant bachelor's or master's background, a department that genuinely matches their subject, acceptable English/Korean evidence and the ability to meet the original-document deadline.
  • Not a sensible last-minute application: applicants whose documents cannot reach Gimhae by 27 November, who have not confirmed department fit, or who are relying on an unapproved MOI waiver.
  • Needs direct academic confirmation: applicants targeting an English-track department where the course language, supervisor availability or research position is unclear.

Live-cycle status checked on 25 November 2026

The Korean government's Study in Korea application receipt page still displayed Inje University's Spring 2027 master's, PhD and combined master's–PhD listings as accepting applications on 25 November 2026. The university's own guide, however, requires the complete original-document package to arrive at the Office of International Affairs by 27 November 2026. The online application period and the physical-document deadline both close on that date.

This is now an urgent, two-part deadline: completing the online form does not complete the application. A courier receipt showing that documents were dispatched is not enough if the originals do not reach Inje by the deadline. Applicants in India should contact the Office of International Affairs immediately to ask whether their documents can realistically arrive on time and whether any delivery arrangement is accepted. Do not assume that email scans, a courier tracking estimate or a late-arrival exception will be accepted.

Official checks: Study in Korea application receipt listings and the Inje University 2027 Spring international graduate admission guide. The university says its schedule may change without prior notice; reconfirm directly before acting.

Before paying the application fee or sending original documents

  1. Confirm the exact department, degree level and whether it is genuinely suitable for your previous qualification.
  2. Ask the department to confirm the current teaching/supervision language and whether your English evidence is accepted. The guide lists IELTS 5.5, TOEFL iBT 71, CEFR B2 and NEW TEPS 326, but a general language threshold does not establish that every class is taught in English.
  3. Confirm the university's original-document checklist, translation, apostille or consular-confirmation requirements for your Indian certificates.
  4. Check that your bank certificate or permitted adviser affidavit meets the guide's issue-date and amount requirements. The listed KRW 16,000,000 financial evidence is an admission-document condition and should not be treated as proof that all separate visa requirements are met.
  5. Ask the university to confirm whether your original package can arrive by 27 November. If it cannot, do not represent the application as complete.

Inje lists the Office of International Affairs at oia@inje.ac.kr and +82-55-320-3605 in its published guide. Include your intended department and degree level in any enquiry so the office can direct the question appropriately.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date for Inje University Spring 2027 graduate admission?

The guide gives 27 November 2026 for the application and arrival of original documents. The original package must reach the Office of International Affairs by that date.

Does Inje University accept IELTS 5.5?

The guide lists IELTS 5.5 or above among its English proficiency routes, alongside TOEFL iBT 71, CEFR B2 and NEW TEPS 326.

Can I apply without TOPIK?

Potentially, if you satisfy one of the listed English routes and your department supports the language arrangement. Confirm the exact department and programme language.

Is the 85% scholarship guaranteed?

No. It requires full-time status, a recommendation, a GPA of at least 3.5/4.5 and field-specific research or academic achievements. Failure to meet the obligations can affect graduation.

How much bank balance does Inje list?

The guide lists a minimum KRW 16,000,000 bank deposit certificate in the applicant's or parent's account, or an affidavit from the expected Inje adviser. It must be issued within 30 days of application.

Does Inje guarantee a Korean student visa or CCVI?

No. The guide describes a student-visa process after admission and payment but does not guarantee a particular visa issuance route or approval.
